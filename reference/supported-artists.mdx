Pick any of these in the `tracker` option of `/tracker`, or the `artist` option of `/snippets` and `/compilation`. Some artists have more than one tracker, shown as separate choices.

Yeat, Playboi Carti, Ken Carson, Destroy Lonely, Kanye West, Drake, Juice WRLD, Lil Uzi Vert, Travis Scott, Future, Young Thug, Gunna, Kendrick Lamar, Don Toliver, OsamaSon, Pop Smoke, Lancey Foux, Pi'erre Bourne, Summrs, slayr, Edward Skeletrix, kash, Sheff G, Dax, Joji, Rihanna and Phoebe Bridgers.

New artists are added regularly. Discord's option list always shows the current set.

## Every other tracker

- **TrackerHub.** Every tracker on TrackerHub is in the same `tracker` list, next to ours. Use `/trackerhub` to search TrackerHub's own list.
- **Any public sheet.** `/tracker load <link>` adds a public Google Sheets tracker to your own list for 7 days, up to 5 at a time.

Want an artist added? Ask in the [support server](https://discord.gg/Yqyh7pVzne).
