A call list — called a **saved team** in Soundcheck — is a reusable staffing template that stores your standard lineup and the order in which to contact each person. Set a primary for every position and as many ordered backups as you need. When you apply the list to a new gig, Soundcheck invites the primaries and puts the backups on standby — ready to step in automatically if someone declines, with no manual promotion required.

<Info>
  Saved teams with call-order priority are a **Pro** feature. You can try them free during your 14-day Pro trial. Manual team building on each gig is still available on the Free plan. [See plan details →](/subscriptions/plans)
</Info>

## What a call list contains

A saved team is made up of **positions** (the roles you need filled) and **members** (the people assigned to each position in priority order). For each position you can define:

- **Call Order 0** — the primary: the first person invited when you apply the list to a gig
- **Call Order 1, 2, 3…** — backups: people added to the gig in standby status, notified automatically if the primary (or a preceding backup) declines

When you apply a saved team to a gig, primaries land as **Invited** and receive the normal invitation flow right away. Backups land as **Standby** — they're on the gig but not messaged until they're needed. If a primary declines, Soundcheck promotes the next standby to pending and notifies them automatically. You don't have to intervene.

Saved teams do not carry pay rates. Set performance fees and payment terms for each gig after you apply.

## Create a call list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Teams page">
    In the web app, navigate to **Organization → Teams**. This is where all your saved team templates live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a new template">
    Select **+ New template** and give the saved team a clear name. Use names that describe the configuration so anyone on your org can tell at a glance which list to apply — for example, "Full Band – 5 Piece" or "Club Night – Trio."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add positions">
    Add each position you need to staff for this lineup. Positions come from your org-level catalog under **Settings → Positions**, so the names stay consistent across gigs, invitations, and imports. Add all the positions that belong in this template before you assign members.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign members with call order">
    For each position, assign your primary (call order 0) and any backups (call order 1, 2…). You can drag to reorder backups after you add them.

    **Example — Full Band, 5 Piece:**

    | Position | Call Order 0 (Primary) | Call Order 1 (Backup) | Call Order 2 (Backup) |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Lead Vocals | Sarah | Jessica | — |
    | Lead Guitar | John | Marcus | Chris |
    | Rhythm Guitar | Alex | John | — |
    | Bass | Mike | Alex | — |
    | Drums | Dave | Tyler | — |

    For critical positions like vocals and drums, two or three backups keep you covered even if your first and second choices are both unavailable. For more flexible roles, one backup is usually enough.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the template">
    Save the saved team. It's now available to apply on any gig in your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Apply a call list to a new gig

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Team on the gig">
    On the gig, select **Build team** (or **Manage Team** if a team is already started). This opens the team builder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the saved team">
    Inside **Manage Team**, select the saved team you want to apply. Positions and members populate automatically from the template. Primaries are marked as Invited; backups are marked as Standby.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set fees and gig-specific details">
    Saved teams don't carry pay rates, so set each member's **performance fee** and any **fee notes** or gig-specific instructions now. This is also a good moment to swap a member if you already know someone's unavailable.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and send invitations">
    Select **Continue**, review the roster on **Review & send invites**, then choose **Save & Send**. Primary invitations go out immediately. Standby backups receive no message at this stage — they're on the roster and ready to be promoted if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Re-applying a saved team to a gig that already has some members won't duplicate people who are already on the roster. Soundcheck only adds members who aren't yet on the gig.
</Note>

## Save an existing gig's roster as a call list

You don't have to build a saved team from scratch. If you've already staffed a gig and want to reuse that lineup, open the **Staffing** header on the gig and select **Save as team**. The team form opens pre-filled with one seat per position and whoever is assigned to each shift (accepted crew first, then pending). Edit call order and backups before you save.

This is the fastest way to create your first saved team — staff one gig the way you normally would, then save it as a template for everything that follows.

## Update call lists as your roster changes

A saved team reflects your roster at the time you apply it. When crew members join your organization, leave, or change their reliability patterns, keep your saved teams current:

<Accordion title="Adding a new crew member to a call list">
  Open the saved team in **Organization → Teams**, find the relevant position, and add the new member at the appropriate call order. If they're your new go-to for that role, set them at call order 0 and move the existing primary to backup 1.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Removing a crew member from a call list">
  Open the saved team, find the member across all positions they appear in, and remove them. Promote any backup above their order if needed to keep the list contiguous. An empty slot at call order 0 means there's no primary for that position when you apply the list — fill it before your next gig.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reordering backups">
  Open the saved team and drag to reorder members within a position's call list. Changes take effect the next time you apply the list; existing gigs are not affected.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Name your saved teams specifically enough that anyone in your org can pick the right one without asking. "Wedding – Full Production (6 Piece + Sound)" is more useful than "Wedding Band." Include the configuration type, size, and any crew roles that make it distinct.
</Tip>
