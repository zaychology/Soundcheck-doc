Your organization is the home for everything in Soundcheck — gigs, setlists, members, positions, leads, and settings. Before you invite your first crew member or create your first gig, take a few minutes to configure your org so every surface (invitations, emails, your booking page) reflects your brand and your workflow.

<Note>
  Only **Owners** and **Admins** can access Organization settings. Find them by opening **Organization** in the sidebar.
</Note>

## Settings overview

Organization settings are organized into tabs. Here's what each one controls:

| Tab | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Name, logo, contact details, timezone |
| **Members** | Roster, invitations, seat usage, onboarding overrides |
| **Positions** | The catalog of roles you staff for |
| **Onboarding** | Steps new members complete when they join |
| **Notifications** | Org-wide notification defaults |
| **Departments** | Group members into bands or departments *(when enabled)* |
| **Connections** | Integrations: QuickBooks, HubSpot, Flex Connect, and payout setup |
| **Billing** | Plan, seats, payment method, and invoices |

## Complete the setup checklist

Work through these steps in order to get your organization fully configured.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your name, logo, and contact details">
    Open **Settings → Organization → Profile**. Your organization name and logo appear on invitations, member-facing emails, and your public booking page — so get these right first.

    - **Name**: how your organization appears everywhere in Soundcheck.
    - **Logo**: upload a PNG or JPG for a polished look on emails and your booking page.
    - **Contact details**: used on member-facing communications and your public booking page.
    - **Timezone**: new organizations start with the creator's detected timezone. Change it here if your org operates in a different zone. Event times fall back to this timezone when a gig has no timezone of its own. Use UTC to keep Coordinated Universal Time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Define your positions">
    Open **Settings → Organization → Positions**. Positions are the roles you staff for — Lead Vocals, FOH Engineer, Tour Manager, Lighting Director. Define them once at the org level and the same names appear on every gig, saved team preset, call list, and onboarding flow.

    Create your positions before you set up onboarding steps, since the Positions onboarding step pulls from this catalog.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure onboarding steps">
    Open **Settings → Organization → Onboarding**. Decide what new members complete when they accept their invitation. Steps can collect:

    - **Profile details** (name, photo, contact info)
    - **Positions** — members pick the roles they can fill from your active position catalog
    - **Custom fields** — any information specific to your org
    - **Documents** — forms or agreements you need signed before someone joins the roster
    - **Phone verification** — when a phone field is required, the member verifies via SMS before continuing

    <Note>
      Each onboarding step supports a single phone-verification field.
    </Note>

    Long-time collaborators who don't need to click through every step? Use the **manual-onboarded override** to mark them complete. See [Managing members](/organization/members#the-manual-onboarded-override).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up payout connections">
    Open **Settings → Organization → Connections**. This is where you wire up your financial connection so you can pay crew directly from Soundcheck.

    Connect and verify a **bank account** (verified accounts are required for ACH payouts) or configure a card for card-funded payouts. The Connections page also shows integration cards for **QuickBooks**, **HubSpot**, and **Flex Connect** even before anything is connected — explore what's available for your plan.

    <Card title="Gig payments" icon="money-check-dollar" href="/features/payments/overview">
      How member payouts work once your financial connection is set up.
    </Card>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review integrations">
    Still in **Settings → Organization → Connections**, connect any external services your workflow depends on:

    - **QuickBooks Online** — sync Soundcheck payouts and income for reconciliation
    - **HubSpot ↔ Flex** — keep deal stages, quotes, and gig status in lockstep
    - **n8n CRM sync** — push external CRM data into Soundcheck via webhook

    <Card title="Integrations overview" icon="plug" href="/integrations/overview">
      See everything Soundcheck connects with.
    </Card>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your core team">
    Open **Settings → Organization → Members**. Invite owners and admins first, then coordinators and members. Mind your plan's seat limit — active members and pending invitations both count against it.

    <Card title="Managing members" icon="users" href="/organization/members">
      Invite members, manage seat limits, and handle onboarding.
    </Card>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first gig or import your schedule">
    You're ready to go. Create a gig from the **Gigs** board, or bring in an existing schedule using the importer at **Settings → Organization → Imports**. The importer accepts crew rosters, venues, clients, songs, and gigs from Planning Center, Back On Stage, or a spreadsheet.

    <Card title="Getting started guide" icon="rocket" href="/getting-started/overview">
      First steps for new organizations — from signup to your first gig.
    </Card>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multi-organization support

You can belong to multiple organizations — multiple bands, several acts, or both performer and crew roles. Use the **organization selector** at the top of the sidebar to switch. All data (gigs, setlists, positions, settings) switches with it, and each org has its own independent subscription. Your role can differ across orgs, and data is fully isolated — members of one org never see another's data.

---

## Managing your subscription

Owners manage the plan and billing from **Settings → Organization → Billing**: view your current plan, upgrade or downgrade, update payment methods, and download invoices. **Manage Subscription** opens the Stripe customer portal.

<Warning>
  Billing lives at **Settings → Organization → Billing**, not on the personal Settings page (which covers appearance only).
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the Connections tab">
    Confirm you are an Owner or Admin and open **Settings → Organization → Connections**. If the tab is missing for your role, ask your account Owner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see the Departments tab">
    Departments is a gated feature and isn't enabled for every organization. Contact support if you need it turned on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My settings won't save">
    Confirm you have an Owner or Admin role and that you're viewing the correct organization using the org selector.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My timezone is showing the wrong zone">
    Open **Settings → Organization → Profile** and update the timezone. Use UTC if you want Coordinated Universal Time. Note that timezone cannot be cleared — UTC is the way to keep a neutral zone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
