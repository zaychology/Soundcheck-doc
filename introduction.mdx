Soundcheck reads the community-run Google Sheets that catalogue an artist's unreleased and released songs, and makes them searchable from Discord. It also finds snippets, downloads media from common links, checks audio files, connects Last.fm, and gives server managers control over where each command works.

## What Soundcheck does

- Searches trackers for 27 artists plus every tracker listed on TrackerHub, and any public tracker sheet you load yourself.
- Finds snippets and compilations, and shows era details with their artwork.
- Downloads media from SoundCloud, TikTok, X and Pillowcase links, and looks up Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube links.
- Checks audio files: spectrograms, tags, metadata, and song identification.
- Shows Last.fm listening, Genius credits, levels, and temporary voice channels.

<Note>
Soundcheck does not download from YouTube. YouTube blocks the servers Soundcheck runs on, so `/youtube` only searches and shows video details.
</Note>
