## Get up and running in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and create your account">
    Go to [app.soundchecklive.io](https://app.soundchecklive.io) and click **Sign up**. Every workspace starts with an organization — your band, production company, or solo act. New accounts start on a 14-day Pro trial so you can explore every feature before choosing a plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your organization">
    Walk through the setup wizard: name your org, pick your business type (which seeds a starter position catalog), and add your logo. Then refine your positions, configure notifications, and verify your timezone under **Settings → Organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first gig">
    Open **Gigs** and click **New Gig**. Add a title, date, and venue. Your gig goes Active immediately — visible to you and any admins — with no separate publish step needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your crew">
    From **Settings → Members**, send email invitations to your team. Each person receives an onboarding prompt in their Mailbox to complete their profile and set their positions. Once they accept, you can assign them to gig positions and send offers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
