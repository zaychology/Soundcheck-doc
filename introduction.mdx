Soundcheck is a Discord bot for music discovery, artist tracking, media tools, and community management. It supports both slash commands and prefix commands where Discord permits them.

The bot combines music-focused features—trackers, snippets, metadata tools, Last.fm, Spotify, SoundCloud, TikTok, X, and YouTube—with practical server features such as configurable commands, welcome messages, logs, levels, emoji management, and temporary voice channels.

## What Soundcheck does

- Finds artist tracker content including released and unreleased music, tracklists, artwork, eras, snippets, live recordings, and more.
- Retrieves song, artist, album, metadata, cover-art, and platform information.
- Connects Last.fm accounts for now-playing status, listening history, charts, and profiles.
- Provides configurable leveling, message logging, automated greetings, roles, and moderation-friendly controls.
- Lets members create and manage their own temporary voice channels through VoiceMaster.

<Note>
Server settings and moderation controls require the relevant Discord permission. Owner-only operational commands are intentionally not documented here.
</Note>
