Use this guide to go from an empty Soundcheck account to a fully operational workspace in one week. Each day has a clear focus and a short action list — you don't need hours, just 20–30 minutes per day. Every time you sign in, you land on **Home**, your personalized starting point. Onboarding prompts for new members appear in **My Mailbox** under the **Onboarding** tab, not on Home itself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day 1 — Set your foundation">
    Get your profile and organization configured correctly before anything else. Settings you set today — especially timezone — affect every event you create.

    **Complete your profile:**
    - Add your full name and a profile photo so teammates recognize you
    - Set the positions (roles/instruments) you can fill
    - Check your notification preferences — org-level notification defaults live under **Settings → Organization → Notifications**

    **Configure your organization:**
    - Go to **Settings → Organization** and verify your org name and logo
    - Set your **timezone** — this drives every event time in your workspace; get it right before creating gigs
    - Skim the other Organization tabs so you know where things live

    <Note>
      Your signup wizard seeded a starter position catalog based on your business type. You'll review and refine those positions tomorrow.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Day 2 — Review your position library">
    Your positions are the roles that need to be filled on every gig — Lead Vocals, FOH Engineer, Stage Manager, and so on. The signup wizard pre-loaded a catalog based on your business type. Today you refine it.

    1. Go to **Settings → Organization → Positions**
    2. Scan the pre-seeded positions and rename any that don't match how your team talks
    3. Add any specialized roles you staff regularly
    4. Remove positions you'll never use

    Accuracy here pays off: every time you staff a gig or send an invitation, you're drawing from this catalog. Clean positions now means faster staffing later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Day 3 — Invite your core team">
    Bring in the people you work with most. Invitations go out by email and each person's onboarding runs through their own Mailbox.

    1. Go to **Settings → Members** and open the **Invite new members** panel
    2. Add email addresses and assign a role for each person:
       - **Admin** — manages gigs, teams, setlists, and most org settings
       - **Gig Coordinator** — handles day-to-day booking operations without billing access
       - **Member** — views and responds to gigs they're assigned to
    3. Send the invitations
    4. Follow up to confirm they received the email and help them finish their onboarding

    <Warning>
      Pending invitations count toward your plan's seat limit. Free plans include 5 seats. If an invite is blocked, free up a seat or upgrade before resending.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Day 4 — Build your setlist">
    Create a setlist you can attach to your first gig. Start with songs you perform regularly so you have real content to work with.

    **Build your song library first:**
    1. Open **Setlists → Song Library** and add 10–20 of your most-performed songs
    2. Include duration, key, and tempo for each — this helps with show pacing
    3. Optionally attach chord charts, lyrics, or backing tracks

    **Then create your setlist:**
    1. Go to **Setlists** and click **New Setlist**
    2. Name it for the show type (e.g., "Standard 90-Min Set" or "Corporate Event")
    3. Add sets — Opening, Main, Encore — and drag songs in from your library
    4. Check the total duration at the bottom

    <Tip>
      Quality over quantity. Fifteen complete song entries beat fifty half-finished ones. You can also import a full album directly from Spotify to seed your library fast.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Day 5 — Send your first contract">
    If you send agreements to clients or crew before shows, set up your contract workflow today so it's ready when you need it.

    1. Open a gig (or create a quick test gig) and navigate to the **Contracts** section
    2. Create a contract — add your terms, fee structure, and any event-specific details
    3. Check that your org name and logo appear correctly (Soundcheck uses your organization branding automatically)
    4. Send it for e-signature directly from the gig

    Once signed, the contract is stored on the gig record and accessible to anyone with permission. You don't need a separate e-signature tool.

    <Note>
      Contracts are white-labeled with your organization's branding — the recipient sees your name and logo, not Soundcheck's.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Day 6 — Create and staff your first gig">
    Put everything together. Create a real (or practice) gig and send invitations to your crew.

    **Create the gig:**
    1. Open **Gigs** and click **New Gig** (or **Create First Gig** on an empty list)
    2. Enter a title, date/time, and venue — location is optional if the hold isn't firm yet
    3. Save. Scratch-created gigs go **Active** immediately with no separate publish step

    **Staff the gig:**
    1. Open **Build Team → Manage Team** on the gig
    2. Add the positions this gig needs and assign people to each
    3. Set fees and any payment notes per position
    4. Click **Continue → Review & Send Invites → Save & Send**

    **Finish the details:**
    - Add a timeline with load-in, soundcheck, doors, and set times
    - Attach your setlist
    - Upload any relevant files (stage plot, rider, venue map)

    **Check for availability first:**
    If you're unsure who's available, send an **Availability Request** before staffing. Go to **People → Availability** to ask your roster about a date. Responses land in Mailbox so you can staff confidently.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Day 7 — Close out and settle the books">
    After your first real show, run through the closeout process to understand how Soundcheck handles post-show settlement. (You can practice this on a test gig if your first live event hasn't happened yet.)

    1. Open the completed gig and click **Mark as Completed** (or wait for Soundcheck to prompt you after the event date passes)
    2. Confirm attendance — note who played and who no-showed
    3. Review and approve crew payouts — each position's fee populates from what you set during staffing
    4. Process payments: crew can receive funds via ACH direct deposit or card
    5. Click **Settle** to move the gig to **Settled** status — the books are reconciled and the record is closed

    Once settled, the gig becomes a clean, permanent record of who performed, what was paid, and how the show went.

    **Make Home and Mailbox your daily routine:**
    - Check **Home** each morning for stats, activity, and your Rounds digest of open items
    - Work **Mailbox** to respond to invitations, crew offers, and availability requests from one inbox
    - Admins get the org-wide **Mailbox** view to see everything the organization has sent

    <Tip>
      Your account starts on a 14-day Pro trial. During your trial, explore Pro features like saved call lists, advanced availability tracking, and financial roll-ups. Before your trial ends, go to **Settings → Billing** to choose the plan that fits your operation. Free, Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans are available.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick reference

| Day | Focus | Key actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation | Profile, org settings, timezone |
| 2 | Structure | Review and refine positions |
| 3 | People | Invite team members |
| 4 | Setlists | Build song library and first setlist |
| 5 | Contracts | Send first agreement for e-signature |
| 6 | Events | Create and staff first gig |
| 7 | Closeout | Settle the gig, establish your daily routine |
