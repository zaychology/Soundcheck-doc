## Organization

An **Organization** is the foundational unit in Soundcheck. It represents your band, entertainment company, production house, or solo act. Everything in Soundcheck — gigs, setlists, crew, contracts, payments, and settings — belongs to an organization.

Key points:
- You can belong to multiple organizations with a different role in each
- Each organization has its own subscription plan and feature access
- Organizations have owners, admins, gig coordinators, and members with different permissions

---

## Gigs

A **Gig** (also called an event) is a scheduled performance or engagement. It's the central object in Soundcheck — everything else attaches to it: the crew, the setlist, the contract, the timeline, the payout.

What a gig includes:
- Event title, type, date, and time
- Venue location (with map integration)
- Team assignments and crew positions
- Attached files (contracts, stage plots, riders)
- A timeline/run of show for the day
- An associated setlist
- Open items — the pending invites, payouts, and tasks still needing action

### Gig statuses

Every gig moves through a lifecycle. The status controls what workflows are available and what the crew can see.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being planned; not yet visible to the crew. Used when creating from a template. |
| **Active** | Confirmed and live. Crew can see the gig and respond to invitations. |
| **Completed** | The show has happened; ready for closeout and payout. |
| **Settled** | Fully closed out. Payouts done and the books reconciled. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off. Kept as a record but no longer in active workflows. |
| **Template** | A reusable gig structure you can duplicate to start future drafts. |
| **Archived** | Completed history tucked out of your main lists without being deleted. |

<Note>
  Scratch-created gigs (built from New Gig) start as **Active** immediately — there's no separate publish step. Only gigs created from a template start as **Draft**.
</Note>

## Tours

A **Tour** is a lightweight folder that groups related gigs together — a run of shows, a season, or a multi-week tour. Tours add a financial roll-up across every gig in the group so you can see the full picture in one place. Each gig optionally belongs to one tour; grouping is flat (no nested tours).

## Positions

A **Position** represents a role or job that needs to be filled for a gig — Lead Vocals, Bass, FOH Engineer, Stage Manager, Lighting Tech, Tour Manager. Positions are defined at the organization level and can be assigned to any gig.

Your signup business type seeds a starter position catalog automatically. Refine it anytime under **Settings → Organization → Positions**.

---

## Team Members

**Team members** are the people associated with your organization who can be assigned to gigs. There are two types:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organization members** | Users with full Soundcheck accounts in your org |
| **External contacts** | People who receive invitations by email only, without a full account |

---

## Engagements

**Engagements** is the umbrella term for anything that lands in a crew member's Mailbox and requires a response. There are four types:

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Invitation** | A request to fill a specific position on a specific gig, with a fee and call order |
| **Crew Offer** | An open staffing offer for a shift; accepted offers add the member to a gig |
| **Availability Request** | A scheduling check asking whether a member is free for a date or window |
| **Onboarding** | A prompt for a new member to finish setting up their profile and positions |

---

## Invitations

An **Invitation** is the most common type of engagement — a direct request sent to a team member to fill a position on a gig. It includes the position, the performance fee, fee notes, and the member's call order (primary or backup).

Invitation statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Sent, awaiting response |
| **Accepted** | Member confirmed |
| **Declined** | Member can't participate |
| **Tentative** | Member indicated possible availability |

## Rounds

**Rounds** is the home-page digest of open items across all your gigs, bucketed by urgency. It rolls up every pending invitation, unsigned contract, unpaid payout, and outstanding task from every gig in your organization — so you see exactly what needs attention today, without opening each gig individually.

---

## Setlists

A **Setlist** is a collection of songs organized for a performance. It attaches to a gig so your entire crew — from the band to the sound engineer — works from the same performance plan.

Structure:
- **Setlist** — the complete performance plan
- **Sets** — groupings within a setlist (Opening Set, Acoustic Set, Encore)
- **Songs** — individual tracks within each set, with title, artist, duration, key, BPM, and optional media links (charts, lyrics, backing tracks)

You build setlists with a drag-and-drop editor. Songs come from your org's **Song Library** — a reusable catalog searchable by title, artist, key, or tempo. You can also import a full album directly from Spotify to seed your library.

---

## Mailbox

**Mailbox** is the single inbox for all engagements. Instead of separate lists for invitations, availability requests, and onboarding prompts, everything lands in one place.

- Every member has a personal **My Mailbox** (accessible from the avatar dropdown).
- Owners, admins, and gig coordinators also get an org-wide **Mailbox** at `/org-engagements` — an aggregate view of everything the organization has sent.

---

## Contracts

Soundcheck's **Contracts** feature lets you send agreements for e-signature directly from a gig. Contracts support white-label branding (your org name and logo) and are sent without leaving the platform. Signed copies are stored on the gig record.

---

## Payments

Crew payments are processed through Soundcheck during the gig closeout. Members can receive funds via **ACH direct deposit** or **card**. Bank accounts are verified with micro-deposits before the first payout.

---

## CRM (Team and Enterprise)

The **CRM** module — available on Team plan and above — tracks the business side of your operation:

- **Customers** — clients who book your services
- **Venues** — locations you perform at or work with
- **Sponsors** — organizations supporting your events financially or in kind (appears as a tab on the Leads Pipeline page)
- **Leads pipeline** — track inquiries from New → Qualified → Quoted → Won/Lost and convert them into booked gigs

<Info>
  Pro does not include Leads. Leads require Team or Enterprise. On Free, the Leads sidebar item appears locked.
</Info>

## User Roles

Soundcheck uses role-based permissions to control what each person sees and can do:

| Role | What they control |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access — settings, billing, members, and can delete the org |
| **Admin** | Gigs, setlists, teams, members, and most org settings; no billing access |
| **Gig Coordinator** | Gig operations, setlists, call lists, availability, and CRM; no billing or payouts |
| **Member** | Views and responds to gigs they're assigned to; limited admin access |

## CheckAI

**CheckAI** is Soundcheck's built-in AI assistant. Ask it questions about your live-event data in plain language — upcoming gigs, pending payouts, crew availability — and it answers from your actual org data.

---

## MCP Server

The **MCP server** exposes your Soundcheck data through the Model Context Protocol, letting you connect AI agents like Claude and Cursor directly to your gigs, crew, and operations.
