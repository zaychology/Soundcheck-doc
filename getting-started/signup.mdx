Getting into Soundcheck starts with creating an organization — your band, production company, or solo act. There's no solo-only account type; every workspace belongs to an org, which means your gigs, setlists, crew, and settings all live in one place from day one. This guide walks you from the signup page to a configured workspace ready for your first gig.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to app.soundchecklive.io">
    Open [app.soundchecklive.io](https://app.soundchecklive.io) in your browser and click **Sign up**. The signup wizard walks you through three steps: your organization, your business type, and your account credentials — in that order.

    <Tip>
      New accounts automatically start a 14-day Pro trial. You get full access to Pro features right away, with no credit card required to begin.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your organization">
    Enter your **organization name**. This name appears on crew invitations, contracts, your booking page, and anywhere clients interact with your brand — so use the name you actually perform or operate under.

    Optionally upload a **logo**. A monogram generates automatically until you add one. You can update both at any time from **Settings → Organization**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your business type">
    Select the type that best describes your operation:

    - **Production company**
    - **Band or artist**
    - **DJ collective**
    - **Venue**
    - **Event producer**
    - **Something else**

    Your choice silently seeds a **starter position catalog** — a set of relevant crew roles pre-loaded into your organization. Production company gets positions like Stage Manager and FOH Engineer; Band or artist gets Lead Vocals, Guitar, Bass, Drums, and so on. You refine this catalog after signup under **Settings → Organization → Positions**.

    <Note>
      The wizard doesn't ask you to review or configure positions. That comes after you're inside the app.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account credentials">
    On the final wizard step, choose how you'll sign in:

    | Method | Available on |
    |---|---|
    | **Email + password** | Web, iOS, Android |
    | **Continue with Google** | Web, iOS, Android |
    | **Continue with Apple** | Web (including Safari), iOS native |

    If you sign up with email and password, Soundcheck sends a verification email. Click the link to verify before continuing. Google and Apple sign-ins verify automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore your Home page">
    Once your account is verified and your organization is ready, you land on **Home** — your personalized starting point. A brand-new workspace shows a **Get started** card with first-run tasks:

    - Create your first gig
    - Invite your team
    - Complete your profile

    Each task checks off as you complete it. Your regular Home view (stats, activity feed, finances) fills in as your org gets data.

    <Info>
      New-member onboarding prompts — like setting your positions and uploading a profile photo — appear in **My Mailbox** under the **Onboarding** tab, accessible from the avatar dropdown in the top navigation.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your organization settings">
    Before inviting anyone, take five minutes to set up your organization properly. Go to **Settings → Organization** and verify:

    - **Name and logo** — confirm both look right
    - **Timezone** — this drives every event time in your workspace; set it correctly before creating gigs
    - **Positions** — review the seeded catalog; rename, add, or remove positions to match how your team actually talks about roles

    Owners and admins can also configure notification defaults under **Settings → Organization → Notifications**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your first team members">
    Invitations happen after signup, not in the wizard. Go to **Settings → Members** and use the **Invite new members** panel to send invitations by email. For each invitee, choose a role:

    | Role | What they can do |
    |---|---|
    | **Admin** | Manage gigs, setlists, teams, and most org settings |
    | **Gig Coordinator** | Manage gig operations and CRM; no billing or payouts access |
    | **Member** | View and respond to gigs they're assigned to |

    <Note>
      As the person who created the organization, you are automatically assigned the **Owner** role — the only role with access to billing, subscription management, and the ability to delete the organization. You cannot invite someone else as Owner; ownership can only be transferred after the fact under **Settings → Organization**.
    </Note>

    Each invitee receives an email with a join link and an onboarding prompt in their Mailbox to complete their profile and set their positions.

    <Warning>
      Pending invitations count toward your plan's seat limit. On Free, you have 5 seats. If an invitation is blocked, free up a seat or upgrade your plan before resending.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's next

With your org set up and your first team members invited, you're ready to create your first gig and start building setlists.
