Soundcheck is a **live event operations platform** that brings your entire gig workflow into one system. You plan events, staff and schedule crew, build setlists, send contracts, process payments, and manage client relationships — all from the same workspace, on web, mobile, and AI assistants.

## The problem Soundcheck solves

Live-event work has always scattered across tools that don't talk to each other: a spreadsheet for gigs, group chats for crew availability, email threads for contracts, and separate apps for payments. When a show changes, you update five places — and someone still shows up with the wrong info.

Soundcheck puts that work in one place. Every gig surfaces its open items — the invites, payouts, contracts, and tasks that still need doing — and **Rounds** rolls them all up across your whole operation, ordered by urgency, so nothing slips before doors.

## Who Soundcheck is for

### Bands and artists
Coordinate members across gigs, assign positions per show, build setlists, and keep everyone on the same page whether you're a duo or a ten-piece.

### Production companies and tour managers
Track events as structured records, manage crew calendars and per-show fees, coordinate multi-week tours with financial roll-ups, and run a full CRM for clients and venues on Team or Enterprise.

### Venues and event producers
Staff positions for each event, manage availability across a roster of freelancers, and maintain a branded client portal for booking inquiries.

### Freelance crew
Accept invitations, read tonight's setlist and run of show from your phone, and receive payouts through Soundcheck — no chasing email threads for the details.

## Your Home page

After you sign in, you land on **Home** — your personalized, org-scoped starting point. Home adapts to your role and plan:

- A **greeting** and quick-action buttons (Create Gig, Add Lead, Invite Team, and more depending on your access)
- **At a glance** stat tiles showing gigs this month, upcoming gigs, pending payouts, and crew responses
- **Rounds** — a digest of open items (pending invitations, unsigned contracts, unpaid payouts, outstanding tasks) bucketed by urgency across every gig in your organization, so nothing slips before doors
- A **recent activity** feed covering gigs, leads, crew offers, and payments
- A **finances** roll-up for roles with financial access on plans that include it

Brand-new workspace? Home shows a **Get started** first-run card that guides you through your first actions — creating a gig, inviting your team, and completing your profile — checking each off as you go.

<Note>
  Onboarding prompts for new members (profile setup, position selection) appear in **My Mailbox** under the **Onboarding** tab, not on the Home page itself.
</Note>

## The core workflow: first inquiry to post-show settlement

Soundcheck follows the natural arc of a live event:

1. **Inquiry → Lead**: A potential booking comes in. On Team and Enterprise, you track it in the CRM leads pipeline and convert it to a gig when it books.
2. **Gig created**: The event exists as a record with a title, date, venue, and timeline. Scratch-created gigs start as **Active** immediately.
3. **Crew staffed**: You add positions, assign team members, and send invitations. Crew respond through Mailbox — accepting, declining, or going tentative.
4. **Show prep**: Setlists are built and attached to the gig. Contracts are sent for e-signature. The run of show is finalized.
5. **Day of show**: The crew works from the shared timeline and setlist. Last-minute changes push via mobile.
6. **Closeout and settlement**: After the show, you move the gig through Completed → Settled. Attendance is confirmed, crew are paid via ACH or card, and the books are reconciled.

## Web vs. mobile

Soundcheck runs on both surfaces with the same account and data — choose what fits the moment:

| When you're… | Use |
|---|---|
| Building gigs, importing data, working leads, configuring your org | **Web** at [app.soundchecklive.io](https://app.soundchecklive.io) |
| On the road, at a venue, accepting an invitation, reading tonight's setlist | **Mobile** (iOS or Android) |

The web app has every feature. Mobile is optimized for read-and-respond use — accepting invitations, checking the setlist, and pushing last-minute changes from the venue floor.

## AI and agents

Soundcheck exposes your live-event data through the **Model Context Protocol** so AI agents can read and act on it directly. **CheckAI** is Soundcheck's built-in AI assistant for querying your operations in plain language. The **MCP server** connects Claude, Cursor, and other agents to your real gig data.
