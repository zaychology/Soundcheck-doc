These four workflows cover the most common paths through Soundcheck, from the moment you have a new date on the calendar to the moment the books are closed. Each one is a practical checklist you can follow step by step.

---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Quick 5-minute gig setup">
    You have a new date. Get from zero to an invited crew in about five minutes.

    1. **Create the gig** — from the Gigs board, click **+ New Gig**. Enter the gig name, date, and event type. Venue and end date are optional at this stage.
    2. **Add a venue** (optional) — search your venue directory or add a new one. Load-in details auto-fill from saved venue records.
    3. **Set call times** — add the schedule (load-in, sound check, downbeat, wrap) under the **Schedule** tab.
    4. **Build the team** — go to the **Team** tab. Add positions and assign crew members from your roster.
    5. **Send invitations** — review the invitation draft and click **Send**. Crew receive an email with a link to accept or decline.
    6. **Attach a setlist** (optional) — go to the **Music** tab and attach an existing setlist or build a new one.

    **Common pitfall:** If your seat limit is full, invitations will be blocked. Check your member count at **Settings → Organization → Members** before sending a batch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Staff a gig from a call list">
    Use a saved call list to fill positions in priority order without manually searching your roster every time.

    1. **Open the gig** and go to the **Team** tab.
    2. **Select a position** that needs to be filled.
    3. **Apply a call list** — click **Use call list** and choose the saved roster for that position.
    4. **Review the order** — Soundcheck shows you the priority list (primary crew, then backups). Adjust if needed.
    5. **Send the invitation** — the system works down the list. The first person to accept fills the slot.
    6. **Monitor responses** — watch the **Open Items** header chip and your Mailbox for accepts and declines. If someone declines, the next person on the list is notified automatically.
    7. **Confirm the filled position** — once the seat is accepted, the gig's team is updated and a payout record is created (if payments are set up).

    **Call lists require Pro or above.** Set them up at **Organization → Call Lists** in the sidebar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lead to booked gig">
    Turn an inbound inquiry into a signed contract and a gig on the calendar.

    1. **Capture the lead** — a prospect submits your public lead intake form, or you manually add them in the **CRM → Leads** pipeline.
    2. **Qualify the lead** — review the inquiry details, add notes, and move the lead through your pipeline stages.
    3. **Convert the lead** — click **Convert** on the lead record. Soundcheck creates a new gig and a customer record simultaneously, and links them.
    4. **Build out the gig** — add the venue, set call times, and configure the team.
    5. **Send a contract** — go to the **Contracts** tab on the gig, prepare the agreement, and send it for e-signature.
    6. **Invite the client to the portal** — once the contract is sent, invite the customer to their **Client Portal** so they can track status, sign, and submit event forms.
    7. **Confirm and activate** — when the contract is signed, publish the gig from Draft to **Active**.

    **CRM and leads require Team or above.** Client portal and contracts are enabled per organization.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Closeout and pay crew">
    After the show: mark the gig done, run the close-out checklist, pay everyone, and settle the books.

    1. **Mark the gig Completed** — after the show date passes, open the gig and move it to **Completed** status.
    2. **Work the close-out checklist** — under the **Closeout** tab, complete each required step: confirm attendance, log any notes, capture reviews. If your org enforces close-out gates, these must be done before you can settle.
    3. **Review the payout roster** — go to the **Finances** tab. Review which crew members have unpaid payout records and confirm they've completed payout onboarding (Stripe Connect).
    4. **Send payments** — initiate payouts via ACH direct deposit or card. ACH takes 2–5 business days to settle.
    5. **Confirm all payouts cleared** — check the payment history in the Finances tab to confirm each payout settled or catch any failures before closing the books.
    6. **Settle the gig** — once all payouts are cleared and the close-out checklist is complete, move the gig to **Settled**. The books are now closed for this show.

    **Payments require a verified bank account** connected at **Settings → Organization → Connections**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
