## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Soundcheck?">
    Soundcheck is a live event operations platform for bands, production companies, and crews. It handles the full gig lifecycle — scheduling, crew staffing, setlists, contracts, payments, and CRM — all in one workspace. It runs on web at [app.soundchecklive.io](https://app.soundchecklive.io) and on iOS and Android.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign up?">
    Go to [app.soundchecklive.io](https://app.soundchecklive.io) and create an account. Soundcheck is org-first: when you sign up, you create an organization (your band, production company, or agency). After verifying your email, you land on your Home page and can start building your first gig or inviting your team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between the free and paid plans?">
    All plans include unlimited gigs, setlists, positions, and member invitations. The differences are in seats and advanced features:

    - **Free**: up to 5 members, core gig management, no call lists or finance roll-ups
    - **Pro**: ~15 members, call lists, finance roll-ups on Home, crew payments
    - **Team**: ~50 members, CRM and leads pipeline, crew availability tools, priority support
    - **Enterprise**: unlimited members and admins, white-label branding, custom onboarding

    See the full [feature comparison](/subscriptions/feature-comparison) for a detailed breakdown.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    The Free plan is free forever — no card required. You only pay when you upgrade to Pro, Team, or Enterprise. Pro and Team upgrades take effect immediately after checkout, and all your Free data stays intact.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I belong to more than one organization?">
    Yes. You can be a member of multiple organizations — multiple bands, several acts, or both a performer and a crew role. Use the **organization selector** at the top of the sidebar to switch. Each org has its own gigs, setlists, positions, settings, and subscription, and data is fully isolated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Gigs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel a gig?">
    Open the gig and look for the **Cancel** action in the gig menu. Only **Draft** and **Active** gigs can be cancelled — gigs that have already completed or been settled are not eligible for cancellation. Before you confirm, Soundcheck shows you what the cancellation will affect (open invitations, unsent payouts, etc.). Cancellation cannot be undone, but the record stays in your history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I duplicate a gig?">
    Open the gig you want to reuse and select **Duplicate Gig** from the gig menu. You'll choose a new date, and Soundcheck copies over the gig details, team structure, setlists, run of show, checklist, and more — times rebase to the new date, and staffing shifts come back as unsent drafts. This is the fastest way to set up a recurring show.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does 'Settled' mean?">
    **Settled** is the final state of a completed gig — it means the show happened, all crew payouts have been cleared, and the books are closed. The gig lifecycle goes: **Draft → Active → Completed → Settled**. A gig can also be **Cancelled** (called off before it happens) or **Archived** (removed from the active view but preserved in your records).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I group multiple shows into a tour?">
    On the **Gigs** board, create a **Tour** folder and add related gigs to it. Tours give you a consolidated view of the run with a financial roll-up (revenue, payouts, and net) across every show inside the folder.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Crew

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do gig invitations work?">
    From a gig's **Team** tab, you select positions and add crew members. Soundcheck sends each person an invitation email with a link they can accept or decline from their Mailbox (web or mobile). You can see each invitation's delivery and response status in real time. Accepted invitations add the crew member to the gig and, if you've set up payments, create a payout record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a call list?">
    A **call list** is a saved, priority-ordered roster that you can drop onto a gig in one click. You define the list once — primary crew, backups, and alternates — and Soundcheck works down the list when staffing. Call lists are available on Pro and above.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a crew member declines an invitation?">
    When someone declines, their seat opens back up and Soundcheck can move to the next person on your call list (if you're using one). You'll see the decline in your Mailbox and in the gig's open items so you can act quickly. You can also resend an invitation or offer the position to someone else manually.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Setlists

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can crew members see the setlist?">
    Yes — crew members assigned to a gig can view the setlist attached to that gig in their Mailbox and on the mobile app. They can also access any chart attachments you've added to individual songs, as long as they've opened them at least once while connected (for offline caching).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Soundcheck support Spotify album import?">
    Yes. When building a setlist, you can import songs from a Spotify album directly into your song library. This populates song names, order, and metadata so you don't have to type everything manually. From there you can reorder, add notes, and attach charts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reuse a setlist across multiple gigs?">
    Yes. Your **song library** is shared across all gigs in your organization. You can save a setlist from one gig and attach it (or a copy of it) to another. Templates and gig duplication also carry over attached setlists.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do payouts take?">
    **ACH direct deposit** typically takes 2–5 business days to settle, depending on your bank and the timing of the transfer. **Card-funded payouts** can be faster. Processing time starts once you initiate the payout from the gig's Finances tab — the crew member's bank or card then determines final delivery time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which countries are supported for payouts?">
    Soundcheck currently supports payouts to crew members with US bank accounts (ACH) or US-issued cards. International payout support is on the roadmap. Contact [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io) if you have specific international needs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I need to set up before I can pay crew?">
    Three things need to be in place: (1) your organization needs a **verified bank account** connected under **Settings → Organization → Connections**; (2) each crew member needs to complete **payout onboarding** from their profile (Stripe Connect); and (3) the crew member needs to have an accepted invitation with an unpaid payout record on the gig. The payout review screen shows the status of each crew member before you initiate a run.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
