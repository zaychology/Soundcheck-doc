Soundcheck generates a private calendar feed for your account in the iCal/ICS format. Subscribe once and your gigs appear alongside everything else in your calendar — no manual exports, no copying dates. The feed updates automatically as gigs are added or changed in Soundcheck, so your calendar always reflects the latest schedule. The feed is **read-only**: you cannot create or edit gigs from your calendar app.

## Find your feed URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the Soundcheck web app, click your profile avatar and go to **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Calendar Sync">
    Under your personal profile settings, select **Calendar sync**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate your feed">
    If no feed exists yet, click **Generate calendar feed**. Your personal subscription link appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your link">
    Copy the subscription URL. The page also offers quick-launch buttons for Apple Calendar and Google Calendar if you want a one-click setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your feed URL contains a private token that acts like a password. Anyone with the link can read the events in your feed. Do not post it publicly. If the URL is accidentally shared, rotate the token (see [Rotating your feed](#rotating-your-feed)) and update your calendar subscriptions with the new URL.
</Warning>

## Add the feed to your calendar app

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Apple Calendar">
    **Option 1 — One-click (macOS or iOS):**

    On the Calendar sync page in Soundcheck, click **Open in Apple Calendar**. Your device opens Calendar and prompts you to subscribe. Accept the subscription and set how often to auto-refresh (every hour is a good default).

    **Option 2 — Manual subscription:**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Copy your webcal URL">
        From the Calendar sync page, copy the subscription link. Soundcheck provides a `webcal://` URL for Apple Calendar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Apple Calendar">
        On macOS, go to **File → New Calendar Subscription**. On iOS, go to **Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste the URL">
        Paste the `webcal://` URL into the server field and tap **Subscribe**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Configure the subscription">
        Give the calendar a name (for example, "Soundcheck Gigs"), set the auto-refresh interval, and confirm. Your gigs appear in the calendar immediately.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Set the refresh interval to **every hour** so new gigs and updates appear in Apple Calendar without delay.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Calendar">
    **Option 1 — One-click:**

    On the Calendar sync page in Soundcheck, click **Open in Google Calendar**. Google opens a confirmation dialog — click **Add calendar**. Your gigs appear in Google Calendar under **Other calendars**.

    **Option 2 — Manual subscription:**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Copy your feed URL">
        From the Calendar sync page, copy the subscription link.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Google Calendar settings">
        In Google Calendar on the web, click the **+** icon next to **Other calendars** in the left sidebar, then select **From URL**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste the URL">
        Paste your Soundcheck feed URL into the **URL of calendar** field and click **Add calendar**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Done">
        Google Calendar imports your gigs and begins polling the feed for updates. The new calendar appears under **Other calendars** in the left sidebar.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Google Calendar controls how often it refreshes subscribed feeds — typically every 12 to 24 hours. You cannot force an immediate refresh from within Google Calendar. For real-time schedule awareness, use the Soundcheck web or mobile app.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Outlook">
    **Desktop (Windows or Mac):**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Copy your feed URL">
        From the Calendar sync page in Soundcheck, copy the subscription link.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the Add Calendar dialog">
        In Outlook, go to your Calendar view. On Windows, select **Add Calendar → From internet**. On Mac, go to **File → New Calendar Subscription**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste the URL">
        Paste your Soundcheck feed URL into the URL field and click **OK** (Windows) or **Subscribe** (Mac).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name the calendar">
        Give the subscription a name such as "Soundcheck Gigs" and confirm. Your gigs appear in Outlook's calendar view.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **Outlook on the web (outlook.com or Microsoft 365):**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Copy your feed URL">
        From the Calendar sync page in Soundcheck, copy the subscription link.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Add calendar">
        In Outlook on the web, click **Add calendar** in the left sidebar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Subscribe from web">
        Choose **Subscribe from web** in the panel that opens.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste the URL and subscribe">
        Paste your Soundcheck feed URL, give the calendar a name, and click **Import**. Your gigs appear in Outlook immediately.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Rotating your feed

Rotate your feed token if the URL was shared with the wrong person, you no longer trust a device or app that has the link, or you want to reset external access for any reason.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Calendar Sync settings">
    Open **Settings → Calendar sync** in Soundcheck.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rotate the token">
    Click **Rotate feed token** and confirm. Soundcheck generates a new URL and invalidates the old one immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your subscriptions">
    Copy the new URL and update each calendar app where you had the old feed subscribed. Subscriptions using the old URL will stop receiving updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What appears in the feed

The feed includes every gig your Soundcheck account can see — any active gig where you are a member or have access through your organization. It reflects the gig's name, date, time, and venue. It is a **gig schedule feed**, not a personal availability feed; your marked availability from Soundcheck does not appear here.
