## CRM & automation

Soundcheck supports several ways to keep your CRM in sync with outside systems. Each option is described below — contact [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io) if you need help enabling any of these for your organization.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="n8n CRM Sync" icon="webhook">
    Push and pull CRM records between Soundcheck and external systems through a signed n8n webhook. Connect any n8n workflow to Soundcheck's CRM events to automate lead creation, contact updates, and record syncing without writing custom code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HubSpot & Flex Pipeline" icon="hubspot">
    Keep HubSpot deal stages, Flex quotes, and Soundcheck gigs in lockstep so nothing falls through between sales and operations. When a deal advances in HubSpot or a quote is accepted in Flex, the matching Soundcheck gig updates automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portal Forms" icon="file-lines" href="/crm/leads">
    Embed branded intake forms on your client portal. Responses flow straight into Soundcheck as leads or event records, so nothing is lost in a shared inbox.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CRM Overview" icon="address-book" href="/crm/overview">
    Manage customers, venues, sponsors, and leads inside Soundcheck — available on Team plan and above.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
