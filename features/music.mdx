Soundcheck plays music in voice channels. Music is a [Premium](/administration/premium) feature.

## Playing a song

1. Join a voice channel.
2. Run `/play` with one of these:
   - **A song name.** Soundcheck searches SoundCloud and plays the best match.
   - **A SoundCloud link** to a song.
   - **An uploaded file.** Use the `file` option (or attach the file to `@Soundcheck play`). Audio and video files up to 100 MB; videos play their sound.
   - **A direct link to an audio file** (MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, OGG and similar, up to 100 MB), or a Pillowcase, imgur.gg, pixeldrain or Google Drive link.
3. Soundcheck joins your channel and posts a now-playing card with a progress bar and player buttons: previous, play/pause, next, stop and **Queue**. Anyone in the channel can use them. Previous goes back to the start of the song, or to the song before if the song only just started.

Run `/play` again while a song is playing to add to the queue (up to 100 songs, each up to 3 hours).

## Songs from trackers

Tracker songs play straight from the links in the sheet: Pillowcase, imgur.gg, pixeldrain, Google Drive and SoundCloud links all work.

- **The Play button.** Song cards from `/tracker search` and the other tracker commands have a **Play** button when one of the song's links can be played. Press it to add the song to the queue in your voice channel.
- **`/play` with a tracker.** Pick a tracker in the `tracker` option, then start typing in `query`: the tracker's songs are suggested. If only one song matches, it plays. If several songs or versions match ([V1], [V2]…), Soundcheck checks their links and the menu lists only the ones that play.
- **Pasting a link.** A Pillowcase, imgur.gg, pixeldrain or Google Drive link in `/play` plays too.

The now-playing card shows the song's era, uses the era's picture and colour like the tracker cards, and links to the song's row in the tracker. Soundcheck tries the song's links in the sheet's order and plays the first one that still works. Links that are down (or on hosts like Mega and exoshare) are skipped, and you're told when none work. Songs without audio ("Not Available", Confirmed, Rumored) have no Play button.

## Other links

YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music links can't be played yet. Use the song's name instead and Soundcheck finds it on SoundCloud. SoundCloud Go+ songs only have a 30-second preview, so they're left out of searches.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/play` | Plays a song, or adds it to the queue. `tracker` plays one from a tracker, `file` plays an upload |
| `/music nowplaying` | Shows the song that's playing, with the player buttons |
| `/music queue` | Shows the songs up next |
| `/music previous` | Back to the start of the song, or to the song before in its first 5 seconds |
| `/music skip` | Skips to the next song |
| `/music pause` | Pauses the music |
| `/music resume` | Carries on after a pause |
| `/music stop` | Stops the music, clears the queue and leaves |

Skip, pause, resume and stop work for members in the same voice channel as Soundcheck.

## Leaving

Soundcheck leaves after 3 minutes with nothing playing, or 30 seconds after everyone leaves the channel. If Soundcheck restarts, it comes back and carries on with the same queue.

## Permissions

Soundcheck needs **Connect** and **Speak** in the voice channel. If it joined your server before music was added, give its role those permissions or invite it again. See [Permissions and setup](/getting-started/permissions).
