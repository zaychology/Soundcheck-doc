---
title: "Last.fm"
description: "Connect a Last.fm account and view listening data in Discord."
---

Connect your account with `/lastfm account login`. You approve Soundcheck on Last.fm's own page; Soundcheck never sees your password. `/lastfm account logout` disconnects it and deletes your saved library and crowns.

## Your listening

| Command | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `/lastfm now` (or `/nowplaying`) | What you're scrobbling now |
| `/lastfm recent`, `/lastfm recentfor` | Recent tracks, or recent tracks by one artist |
| `/lastfm top artists`, `albums`, `tracks` | Your top charts for a period |
| `/lastfm top tenalbums`, `tentracks` | Your top ten albums or tracks by an artist |
| `/lastfm plays artist`, `album`, `track`, `all`, `total` | Your play counts |
| `/lastfm overview`, `streak`, `milestone` | Artist stats, listening streak, a scrobble milestone |
| `/lastfm taste` | Compare your taste with another member |
| `/lastfm collage`, `favorites`, `recommendation`, `link` | Album collage, liked tracks, a random pick, song links |
| `/lastfm account whois` | A Last.fm profile |
| `/lastfm config color` | The colour of your Last.fm cards |

Most commands take an optional `member`, so you can see another member's listening once they've connected their account.

## Leaderboards

`/lastfm account update` saves your library so you show up on leaderboards (it also runs when you log in).

| Command | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `/lastfm global whoknows`, `whoknowsalbum`, `whoknowstrack` | Top listeners on Soundcheck across every server |
| `/lastfm whoknows artist`, `album`, `track` | Top listeners in this server. The #1 listener of an artist (30+ plays) holds its 👑 crown |
| `/lastfm board crowns`, `mostcrowns` | Your crowns in this server, and who has the most |
| `/lastfm playing` | What members of this server are scrobbling right now |
| `/lastfm board server`, `global` | Votes on now playing cards |
| `/lastfm hide add`, `remove`, `list` | Server managers hide a member from this server's boards |

<Note>
The server boards (`whoknows`, `crowns`, `playing`) need Discord's Server Members access to know who is in your server. Until Discord grants it, they say they aren't available yet. The global boards work now.
</Note>
