## Information

`/information` has `server`, `user`, `bot`, `channel`, `role`, `membercount`, `avatar`, `banner`, `serveravatar`, `serverbanner`, `guildicon`, `guildbanner` and `splash`. `/ping` and `/uptime` check the bot.

## Emojis

`/emoji` shows an emoji large. `/emoji list` lists the server's emojis. Members with Manage Expressions can use `/emoji add`, `rename` and `delete`.

## Grails, games and more

- `/grail` shows your or another member's grails.
- `/games` has `8ball`, `coinflip`, `connect4`, `randomnumber`, `rockpaperscissors`, `tic-tac-toe` and `uselessfact`.
- `/translate` translates text.
- `/weather` shows the weather for a place.
- `/topcommands` shows the most used commands.
