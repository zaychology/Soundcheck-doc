## Information

`/info` has `server`, `user`, `bot`, `avatar`, `banner`, `serveravatar` and `serverbanner`. `/ping` checks the bot.

## Trackerdle

A daily guess-the-song puzzle for every tracker. `/trackerdle` opens today's board, and you have 8 guesses. Each guess shows hints for era, credits, when it surfaced, type, availability and length. All versions of a song count as one guess, and everyone gets the same song each day (new one at midnight New York time).

- `/trackerdle guess` makes a guess. **Share** posts a spoiler-free grid of your result.
- `/trackerdle stats` shows your games, wins and streaks. A board you start and don't finish counts as a loss.
- `/trackerdle leaderboard` ranks everyone who plays in the server.
- `/trackerdle history` shows your past boards, or a tracker's past answers.

## Grails, games and more

- `/grail` shows your or another member's grails.
- `/games` has `8ball`, `coinflip`, `connect4`, `randomnumber`, `rockpaperscissors`, `tic-tac-toe` and `uselessfact`.
- `/topcommands` shows the most used commands.
