## Information

`/info` has `server`, `user`, `bot`, `avatar`, `banner`, `serveravatar` and `serverbanner`. `/ping` checks the bot.

## Trackerdle

A daily guess-the-song puzzle for every tracker. `/trackerdle play` opens today's board, you have 8 guesses, and each guess shows hints for era, credits, when it surfaced, type, availability and length. When you finish, **Play another** keeps you going with random songs. See [Trackerdle](/features/trackerdle) for how to play, what every hint means, versions, stats and the leaderboard.

## Grails, games and more

- `/grail` shows your or another member's grails.
- `/games` has `8ball`, `coinflip`, `connect4`, `randomnumber`, `rockpaperscissors`, `tic-tac-toe` and `uselessfact`.
- `/topcommands` shows the most used commands.
