The client portal gives each of your clients a dedicated, branded space where they can act on their events without you chasing them over email. Invite a client once, and from then on they have a single place to sign agreements, review what's owed, fill out intake forms, and follow updates from your team — all organized around their own events. Every client's portal maps directly to their record in your CRM, and what they see is scoped to their customer: their events, their contracts, their invoices, their forms. Nothing bleeds across to other clients.

<Info>
  The client portal rolls out per organization. If you don't see portal actions on your customers, contact [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io) to enable it. Portal invitations require the **Team** plan or above.
</Info>

## What the portal is

The portal is a separate, client-facing workspace — distinct from your staff workspace. Inside it, your gigs are labeled **Events**, the client-facing word for the same thing. The portal carries your organization's name, logo, and colors so it reads as an extension of your business.

## What clients can do

Once inside, a client can take action without going anywhere else:

- **Sign contracts** — contracts you've sent for signature appear on the Contracts page and on each event's Contracts tab. The client selects **Review & sign** and completes the signing window right inside the portal.
- **Review invoices** — the Invoices page gives the client a plain view of deposits, balances, and payment requests, each with an amount, a due or paid date, and a status.
- **Fill out forms** — forms you've assigned to a client appear on the Forms page and on the relevant event. The client selects **Fill out** and completes the form embedded in the portal; responses go straight to your team.
- **Follow event updates** — each event has a Messages area where your booking team can share updates. The portal's Messages page routes the client into whichever event they want to catch up on.

<Note>
  Clients only ever see what's tied to their own customer record. You control what appears by linking gigs to the customer and assigning forms and contracts. When in doubt about what a specific client will see, preview their portal before sending the invitation.
</Note>

## Inviting a client

You invite a client from their **customer record** in your CRM — not from a gig directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer record">
    Go to **Business → Customers** and open the client you want to invite.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the portal">
    On the **Overview** tab, find the **Primary Contact** card and select **Preview portal**. Confirm exactly what the client will see before sending the invitation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    When everything looks right, select **Invite to portal**. The primary contact receives a secure invitation to set up their sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The invitation goes to the **primary contact**, not the customer record as a whole. If you need to invite a different person, make them the primary contact first.

## Managing portal content

What a client sees in their portal depends on the work you've already done in Soundcheck — there's no separate "portal" setup beyond the invitation:

- **Events** appear in the portal when a gig is linked to the customer record.
- **Contracts** appear when you send a contract to the client's contact from within a gig.
- **Invoices** appear when you share an invoice with the customer.
- **Forms** appear when you assign an intake form to the customer or one of their events.

Because everything flows from your CRM and gig records, converting leads, linking gigs, and assigning forms is what populates the portal automatically. You don't need to publish or push anything separately.

## How portal access connects to the CRM

The portal is tightly mapped to your CRM customer record:

- You invite the **primary contact** on a customer record.
- When they accept, their account is bound to that contact and customer.
- The portal then shows only that customer's events, contracts, invoices, and forms.

Because the mapping runs through your CRM, the work you already do — converting leads, linking gigs to customers, assigning forms — is what populates each client's portal automatically.

<Tip>
  Pair the client portal with [white-label contract branding](/features/contracts/overview) for a fully on-brand client experience: your logo and colors carry through both the portal and the contract signing window, so everything the client touches looks like it came from you. White-label branding is available on the **Enterprise** plan.
</Tip>
