A gig's life doesn't end when the show does. **Closeout** is the back half of the event lifecycle: the gig completes automatically once the date passes, you work through a short **close-out checklist**, you pay your crew from the Finances tab, and you **settle** the gig once every payout has cleared. This page walks you through the full process from Completed to Settled.

Closeout lives on the **Closeout** tab of the gig detail, which appears for managers on gigs that have taken place. Settling itself happens from the **status pill** in the gig header.

## How a gig moves to Completed

You never manually mark a gig *Completed*. Once the event date passes, Soundcheck moves the gig into the Completed phase automatically. The status pill updates to **Completed**, the gig drops out of active operations, and the Closeout tab becomes your working view for everything that follows.

## The close-out checklist

The **Closeout** tab shows a checklist of the steps that finish a gig financially. Typical items include:

- **Contract signed**
- **Quote approved**
- **Deposit received**
- **Invoice sent**
- **Payment received** (full client payment)

Each row shows its current state on the right:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Done** | Checked off. |
| **Blocking** | Incomplete and holding up close-out (when your org enforces the checklist). |
| **Blocks settle** | Incomplete and holding up settlement specifically — for example, client payment not yet received. |
| **Tracked, not blocking** | Incomplete, recorded for visibility, but not stopping anything. |
| **Optional** | Never blocks. |

Above the checklist, a **stage chip** tells you where the gig sits — for example, *Delivered · awaiting payment* or *Ready to settle* — and a **days-in-stage** counter flags a gig that has been stuck in the same stage for too long (it turns red after a week).

<Info>
  Whether the checklist actually **blocks** settlement is an organization setting. When enforcement is off, the steps still appear and track your progress, but they don't gate settling. Crew payouts are always managed on the **Finances** tab — they are separate from the close-out checklist and must be cleared before you can mark the gig Settled.
</Info>

## Pay crew from the Finances tab

Before you can settle a gig, every crew payout must clear. Payouts live on the gig's **Finances** tab, not the Closeout tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Finances tab">
    Navigate to the gig and select the **Finances** tab. You'll see a list of accepted crew members with their accepted performance fees as payable items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm attendance">
    Review who actually worked the show. Remove or adjust any crew who didn't perform so your payout list reflects reality.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send payouts">
    Select the crew members to pay and initiate payment by **ACH direct deposit** or **card**. Crew must have a verified payout method on their profile to receive direct deposit. See [Gig payments](/features/payments/overview) for the full payout workflow, including bank account verification with micro-deposits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for payouts to clear">
    ACH transfers typically take 1–3 business days. The payout row updates its status as it processes. Once all payouts show as cleared, the gate for settling is lifted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You cannot mark a gig **Settled** until every crew payout has cleared on the Finances tab. If any payouts are still processing, the settle option reads *"Settle all payouts in Finances first."* The close-out checklist does not control payout clearance — those are two separate gates.
</Warning>

## Mark the gig as Settled

Once every payout has cleared and your close-out checklist is complete (if enforced by your org), you're ready to settle.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm all checklist items are done">
    Review the Closeout tab. Every blocking item should show **Done**. If any items are still outstanding and your org enforces the checklist, complete them or use the admin override (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the status pill">
    In the gig header, tap the **Completed** status pill to open the status menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Settled">
    Choose **Settled**. Soundcheck verifies that payouts have cleared and the checklist is satisfied, then closes the gig. The status pill updates to **Settled** and the gig is fully closed on the books.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you settle by mistake, an Admin or Gig Coordinator can **un-settle** the gig back to Completed from the same status pill.
</Note>

### Admin override for blocked checklists

Sometimes you need to close a gig before every paperwork step is caught up. Organization owners and admins can override a blocked checklist:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attempt to settle with blocking items outstanding">
    The override dialog opens instead of settling directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a reason">
    Provide a written reason for settling early. This field is required — the reason is logged permanently on the Closeout tab, not just noted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Override & settle">
    The gig settles, and the override is recorded: *"Settled with override by [name], '[reason]'"* with a date and time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The admin override skips the **close-out checklist**, not the money gate. Crew payouts must still be cleared on the Finances tab before settling. The override only forgives incomplete paperwork steps — it cannot bypass uncleared payouts.
</Warning>

## Capture reviews

Closeout is also where you record how the gig went. The **Reviews** section on the Closeout tab lets you leave a star rating and notes for:

- **The overall event**
- **The venue** (when the gig has one linked)
- **Each team member** who accepted and worked the gig

Reviews captured here become part of the gig's permanent history and build the reputation record that follows crew members and venues into future work.

## Archive a settled gig

Once a gig is **Settled**, it stays visible on your Gigs board with a Settled status filter. If you want to move old gigs out of your active view entirely, you can **Archive** them. Archiving is a legacy filing state — use it for delivered gigs you want to store without deleting the record. Archived gigs can be found by setting the status filter to **Archived** on the Gigs board.

## Cancelled gigs

Not every gig reaches Settled. A gig that gets called off is **Cancelled** — a status you set from the header pill while the gig is still a Draft or Active. Cancelling is never gated by the close-out checklist, and Soundcheck shows you what the cancellation will affect before you confirm. See the [event lifecycle reference](/getting-started/key-concepts) for the full Cancelled vs. Archived distinction.
