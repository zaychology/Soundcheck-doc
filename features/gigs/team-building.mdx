Staffing a gig means assigning the right people to the right positions before the show. Soundcheck gives you two ways to do it: classic **invitations** you build and send to a named lineup, or **crew offers** that let eligible people respond to an open shift. This page covers both paths — from defining positions to tracking every accept and decline.

## Positions come first

Every seat you staff is tied to one of your organization's **positions**: Lead Vocals, Guitar, Sound Engineer, Stage Manager, and so on. Positions are defined once under **Settings → Positions** and reused across every gig, invitation, and crew offer. That consistency is what makes staffing history and the cross-gig staffing grid work.

<Tip>
  Set up your standard positions before you staff your first gig. New organizations get a starter set of positions at signup based on their business type. Add, rename, or deactivate positions at any time from **Settings → Positions**.
</Tip>

## Two ways to staff a gig

| | Invitations | Crew offers |
|---|---|---|
| **Section is called** | **Team** | **Crew** |
| **You assign** | A specific person to each position | A shift that eligible people respond to individually |
| **Best for** | A known lineup | Filling open shifts or offering work to a shortlist |
| **Enabled by** | The Team invitation flow | The crew availability & offers feature |

If you're not sure which your organization uses, check the section heading on the gig's Details tab: **Team** or **Crew**.

## Path 1: Invitations

Use invitations when you already know who's filling each position. You build the lineup in **Manage Team**, review it, and send all invitations together.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Team">
    On the gig's **Details** tab, find the **Team** section and choose **Build team** or **Manage Team**. On Pro and Team plans, you can apply a **saved Team preset** here to populate the lineup instantly instead of building from scratch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add positions and assign people">
    Choose **Add position** for each role you need to fill. For each position, choose **Add Person** and search for someone from your org roster or add an external contact by name and email. Set the **performance fee** and any **fee notes** for each seat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set call order for backups">
    For critical positions, add more than one person in **call order**: the primary (call order 0) and one or more backups (1, 2…). If the primary declines, the next person in line is up. This keeps a position covered without manual follow-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the lineup">
    When the roster looks right, choose **Continue** to reach the **Review & send invites** screen. Confirm the team, check fees, and make any last adjustments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitations">
    Choose **Save & Send**. Invitations go out to everyone at once. Each recipient receives a notification in their **Mailbox** and, if they have Google Calendar connected, an auto-RSVP on the matching calendar event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After the first batch is sent, the gig's team is live. Add new people from the Team section and send their invitations as you go — you don't need to resend to the whole team.
</Note>

### Invitation statuses

Track every response directly in the Team section of the gig detail.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Invitation sent; waiting for a response. |
| **Accepted** | The crew member confirmed the gig. |
| **Declined** | The crew member can't make it. The next person in call order moves up. |
| **Tentative / Standby** | The crew member responded with conditional availability. |

You're notified as people respond, and the **Team confirmed** progress bar on the gig card updates in real time.

### Resending an invitation

If someone hasn't responded, you can resend their invitation from the Team section. Find their row, open the actions menu (**⋯**), and choose **Resend invitation**. This nudges them without affecting anyone else on the team.

## Path 2: Crew offers

Use crew offers when you're filling an open shift and don't yet have a specific person in mind, or when you want to offer a role to a shortlist and let the fastest responder fill it.

### Two offer types

| Type | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Open shift** | Posted to several eligible people; fills as they accept, up to the number of slots you set. |
| **Job offer** | Sent to a targeted shortlist; every recipient can accept. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the offer">
    From the gig's **Crew** section, choose to create a new offer. Set the **position**, **date and time**, **fee**, **recipient list**, and an optional **expiration date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as draft or send">
    Choose **Save as draft** to hold the shift without notifying anyone yet, or **Review & send** once call times and recipients are confirmed. Recipients don't see a draft in their Mailbox until you send it. Choose **Save & Send** to open the offer and notify recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track each recipient">
    Every recipient is tracked individually with their own response status and response time. The gig's Crew section shows all recipients in one view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more crew to a live offer">
    On a live offer, choose **Send to more crew members** at any time to expand the recipient list. New recipients are notified; people already on the offer are not re-notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  An accepted crew offer with a positive fee creates a payable on the gig's Finances tab. Owners and Admins manage offer payouts; Gig Coordinators handle operational staffing without P&L or payout access.
</Warning>

## Fees and payouts

The performance fee on any accepted invitation or offer becomes a payable item in the gig's **Finances** tab. From there, owners and admins can pay crew by direct deposit (ACH) or card, or record a manual payment.

## Making changes after sending

- **Replace someone**: assign a new person to the position from the Team or Crew section. The previous assignee receives a cancellation notice.
- **Update fee or notes**: edit the assignment and save. Changes apply going forward.
- **Remove someone**: remove them from the gig from their row's actions menu. They receive a cancellation notice.
- **Reprice a shift**: pay freezes at the time of sending, since that's the deal people accepted. To change the fee, cancel and re-issue the offer. You can still set a custom rate for someone you add to a live offer before they accept.
- **Move a call time**: change it in place. Everyone still attached is notified, and anyone who already accepted can decline the new time.

## The cross-gig staffing grid

When the crew availability and offers feature is enabled, managers get a **staffing grid** on the Staffing tab of the People workspace. It shows your org positions as rows and your upcoming gigs as columns, so you can spot an unstaffed position across every show at once without opening each gig individually. Cells also surface available members who said they're free that date and hold that position.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A crew member isn't receiving invitations">
    Check that their email address is correct, ask them to check their spam folder, and confirm they haven't unsubscribed from Soundcheck notifications.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can't find someone to assign">
    Confirm they're a member of your organization and that you have permission to staff gigs. Try searching by email address instead of name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A position isn't showing in the list">
    Positions come from your org catalog. Add or reactivate the position under **Settings → Positions** first, then return to the gig to assign it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Need to apply a pre-built team lineup">
    On Pro and Team plans, open **Manage Team** on the gig and apply a **saved Team preset**. This populates all positions and primary members with backup call order in one step. See [Call lists](/organization/call-lists).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
