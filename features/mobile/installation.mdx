The Soundcheck mobile app is free to install and uses the same account as the web app — no separate signup required. Whether you're on iPhone or Android, you'll be up and running in a few minutes.

**Minimum OS versions:**
- **iOS:** 17.6 or later
- **Android:** Android 10 (API level 29) or later

---

## Install and sign in

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS (App Store)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find Soundcheck in the App Store">
        Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad. Tap the Search tab and search for **Soundcheck**. Look for the app by **Soundcheck Live**.

        [Open on the App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/app/soundcheck-live)
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install the app">
        Tap **Get**, then **Install**. You may be prompted for Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password. Wait for the download to complete.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Grant notification permissions">
        When the app launches for the first time, it will ask permission to send you notifications. Tap **Allow** to receive push alerts for gig invitations, crew responses, and schedule changes. You can adjust this later in **Settings → Soundcheck → Notifications** on your device.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        On the sign-in screen, use the same account you use on the web:
        - **Email + password**
        - **Google**
        - **Apple Sign In**

        If you belong to multiple organizations, pick which one to open first. You can switch later from the **You** tab.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      If the App Store won't let you install Soundcheck, your iOS version may be too old. Update from **Settings → General → Software Update**.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android (Google Play)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find Soundcheck in Google Play">
        Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android device. Search for **Soundcheck**. Look for the app by **Soundcheck Live**.

        [Open on Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.soundchecklive.app)
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install the app">
        Tap **Install**. The download starts automatically. Wait for it to complete, then tap **Open** from the Play Store or find the Soundcheck icon in your app drawer.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Grant notification permissions">
        On Android 13 and above, the app will ask permission to send you notifications. Tap **Allow** to receive push alerts for invitations, crew responses, and gig updates. You can change this in your device **Settings → Apps → Soundcheck → Notifications**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        On the sign-in screen, use the same account you use on the web:
        - **Email + password**
        - **Google**

        <Note>
          Apple Sign In is not available on native Android. If you signed up with Apple on iOS, use email + password or link a Google account from your profile on the web app first.
        </Note>

        If you belong to multiple organizations, pick which one to open first. Switch organizations later from the **You** tab.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      If Google Play won't let you install Soundcheck, your Android version may be too old. Update from **Settings → System → Software update**.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Permissions Soundcheck asks for

| Permission | When you'll be asked | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | First launch | Push alerts for invitations, chat, crew responses, and gig changes |
| **Camera** | When you take a profile photo or attach a photo | Capturing photos in-app |
| **Photo Library** | When you choose an existing photo | Selecting photos for your profile, gigs, or chats |
| **Microphone** | When you record audio for a gig or rehearsal note | Optional voice recording on attachments |
| **Location** | When you use location-aware flows | Gig and venue location context |

You can change any permission at any time in your device's system settings under **Soundcheck**.

## Keeping the app up to date

App updates ship through the App Store and Google Play. Enable **automatic updates** on your device so you always have the latest features and fixes. Older versions may stop receiving certain push notifications as the platform evolves.

## Troubleshooting sign-in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the verification email">
    Check your spam folder for an email from Soundcheck. Add `notifications@soundcheck.live` to your contacts so product emails aren't filtered. If you still don't see it, request a new code from the sign-in screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Google or Apple sign-in fails">
    **Google (iOS and Android):** Confirm you're signed in to the right Google account on your device.

    **Apple (iOS only):** Confirm you're signed in to the correct Apple ID. Android native does not support Apple sign-in.

    If you previously signed up with email, sign in with email first, then link Google or Apple from your profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm in the wrong organization after signing in">
    Open the **You** tab and tap **Switch organization**. If the organization you expect isn't listed, ask an Owner to confirm your membership or re-send the invitation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app won't install">
    Check that your device meets the minimum OS version (iOS 17.6+ or Android 10+) and that you have enough free storage. If your OS is up to date and you still can't install, contact support at info@soundchecklive.io.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
