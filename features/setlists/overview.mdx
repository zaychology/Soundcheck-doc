The Music Management workspace brings your entire performance plan together: a drag-and-drop setlist builder, a centralized song library, and direct links to your gigs so every crew member walks in knowing exactly what's on stage tonight. Whether you're running a wedding, a club residency, or a festival set, Soundcheck keeps your music organized and on time.

## The Music Management workspace

Open **Music Management** from the main navigation to find everything in two tabs:

- **Setlists** — your full list of templates and gig-attached setlists, each showing song count, set count, and total running time. Filter by All, Templates, or Gigs, or search by name.
- **Song Library** — your organization's catalog of performable songs. Add, edit, import from Spotify, and attach charts, lyrics, or media links to any song.

Every setlist card badges its type (template or gig) at a glance, so you always know what's reusable and what's tied to a specific show.

## Setlists vs. templates

A setlist comes in two flavors. Choose the right one when you create it — you can always duplicate later.

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Template** | A reusable shape not tied to any show — a "wedding reception set" or "club night three-set" you stamp out for each new booking. |
| **Gig setlist** | A setlist attached to a specific event. The team assigned to that gig sees it on-site, including on mobile. |

<Tip>
Build your first setlist as a template, then duplicate it for each new show. You'll keep a clean master and spend minutes — not hours — prepping each event.
</Tip>

## Structure: Setlist → Sets → Songs

Every setlist breaks down into sets, and each set holds songs. Soundcheck totals the running time automatically at every level — each set sums its songs, and the setlist sums its sets.

```text
Setlist: "Friday Night at Blue Note"
├── Set 1: Opening  (45 min)
│   ├── Take Five
│   └── Autumn Leaves
├── Set 2: Main  (60 min)
│   └── All Blues
└── Set 3: Encore  (15 min)
    └── A Night in Tunisia
```

You always know whether you fit the slot before you leave for the venue.

## Drag-and-drop reordering

The setlist builder is fully drag-and-drop. Grab any song and move it within its set or into a different set entirely. Drag set headers to reorder the sets themselves. Running time recalculates instantly as you rearrange, so you can adjust energy flow and timing at the same time.

## Running time auto-calculation

Enter a duration for each song in the Song Library (in MM:SS format) and Soundcheck does the math for you. Every set shows its running total, and the setlist header shows the grand total. Songs without a duration entered display as `—:—` and are excluded from the sum — fill in the library and the numbers stay accurate.

## Attaching setlists to gigs

Link a setlist to a gig from either direction:

- **From Music Management** — open the setlist, then choose a gig to attach it to.
- **From the gig detail page** — go to the **Setlist** section and select an existing setlist or create a new one right there.

Once linked, the setlist lives inside the gig so your entire assigned team can pull it up on the day without digging through Music Management.

## Mobile access for crew

Crew members assigned to a gig can open that gig's setlist on iOS or Android. No searching, no spreadsheets — the set order, song details, and any per-song notes are right there when doors open. Mobile setlist access requires the feature to be enabled for your organization; if your team can't see it, ask an admin to turn it on.

## Who can manage setlists

| Action | Owner | Admin | Gig Coordinator | Member |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| View a gig's setlist | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | If assigned to the gig |
| Create / edit setlists | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | No |
| Manage the song library | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | No |
