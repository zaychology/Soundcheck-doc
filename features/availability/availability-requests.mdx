Availability requests let you ask specific crew members whether they're free for a date or time window before you build a gig team. Instead of texting people individually and chasing replies across email and SMS, you send one request, collect responses in one place, and staff from what you see — all without leaving Soundcheck.

<Info>
  Crew availability and offers are enabled per organization. If you don't see the **People** workspace or an option to send availability requests, the feature may not be turned on for your team yet. Contact your organization owner or reach out to [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io).
</Info>

## When to use availability requests

Use an availability request any time you need an explicit confirmation before committing to a date or lineup — especially when standing availability marks alone aren't enough. Common situations include:

- A gig date has just been confirmed and you need to know who's free before you staff it
- Your regular crew has been busy lately and you're not sure who's available
- You want to check a specific time window (load-in through set-end) rather than just the full day
- A critical position has multiple candidates and you want to lock in the best available option first

Availability requests are also the right tool when you need a documented yes/no for a specific event rather than a general calendar mark. Responses reconcile into each member's personal availability calendar automatically, so the crew calendar stays accurate.

## Who can send requests

Owners, admins, and gig coordinators can create and send availability requests from the **People** workspace (**Availability → People** on the web app). Members can only respond to requests that have been sent to them.

## Send an availability request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the People workspace">
    Go to **Availability → People** in the web app. Select **Request availability** to open the request builder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the date and time window">
    Give the request a **title** so recipients know what gig or date you're asking about. Then choose the **date** and select a **mode**:

    - **Whole day** — use this for general date holds when call time doesn't matter yet
    - **Time window** — use this when you need coverage for a specific window, such as 4:00 PM – midnight load-in through end of show

    For a time window, set the **start** and **end times** to match the actual working hours you need covered.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter by position (optional)">
    Use the **position filter** to narrow candidates to only the roles you need — keys, FOH engineer, stage manager, and so on. This ensures the request only reaches people who could actually fill the slot, and keeps your crew's Mailbox relevant.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link a gig (optional)">
    If the request is tied to a specific event already in Soundcheck, link the gig. This gives recipients context and lets you act on responses directly from the gig's staffing view later.

    You can also set an **expiration date** so the request closes automatically if you need a response by a deadline.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose recipients and send">
    Select recipients from your active organization members. You can add individuals or pick from the filtered position shortlist. When your list is ready, send the request. Soundcheck delivers it to each recipient's **My Mailbox** under the **Availability** tab, and also sends an email notification with a one-tap response link.

    If you want to save the request without notifying anyone yet, skip adding recipients — the request stays as a draft until you're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How crew members respond

Recipients see the availability request in their **Mailbox** under the **Availability** tab, alongside any invitations and crew offers. They can also respond directly from the email notification, which deep-links to the right item in Mailbox — no login hunt required.

Each recipient responds with one of three answers:

| Response | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Available** | Free for the date or time window as requested |
| **Unavailable** | Cannot make it; may include an optional note |
| **Tentative** | Uncertain — useful when crew members have a soft conflict they're resolving |

Responses are reconciled straight into each member's personal availability calendar the moment they reply. No extra approval step, no manual updating.

<Note>
  Only the recipient of a request can respond to it. If you need to update a response on someone's behalf, reach out to them directly and have them respond through their Mailbox.
</Note>

## View responses in the org-wide pipeline

As responses come in, you can track them in two places:

- **People workspace → Availability requests**: the full list of sent requests with per-request response counts and individual member answers
- **Org Mailbox → Availability tab**: aggregate response counters across all outstanding requests — useful for a quick check of how many crew members have responded without opening each request

Both views update in real time as recipients reply.

## Act on responses to staff your gig

Once you have enough responses to make staffing decisions, you have two paths:

**Staff directly from the request**: if you linked a gig when you created the request, open the gig's **Manage Team** view. Members who responded **Available** appear as candidates for their matching positions, so you can fill slots without switching screens.

**Use the crew calendar**: the crew calendar reflects all availability responses — mark the date and see each member's status color-coded across positions. Filter by position to narrow the view to the role you're filling, then open **Manage Team** to assign.

<Tip>
  Keep your availability requests focused. Use the position filter so you're only asking people whose role actually matches the gig. A tight, relevant request gets faster responses than a broad one that goes to your whole roster.
</Tip>
