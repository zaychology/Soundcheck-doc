Soundcheck handles crew payments end to end: connect your organization's bank account, let crew members add their own payout destination, then send payments straight from the gig that generated them. Every payout is recorded against the gig, visible in your org-level Payments page, and traceable to settled.

<Info>
  Payment features are enabled per organization and per plan. Payments are available on **Pro and above**. If you don't see the pages described here, payouts aren't turned on for your organization yet — contact [support@soundcheck.live](mailto:support@soundcheck.live).
</Info>

## How payments work in Soundcheck

Two one-time setups unlock routine payouts:

1. **Your organization connects and verifies a bank account.** That verified account funds ACH direct-deposit payouts.
2. **Each crew member onboards their own payout destination** — a bank account for direct deposit, a Stripe Connect account, or both. Members manage this themselves from their own profile; you never touch their account details.

After that, paying the crew is a matter of opening the gig's **Finances** tab, reviewing the payable rows, and sending.

## Two payout methods

| Method | Funded from | Cost to your org | Typical arrival |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Direct Deposit (ACH)** | Your connected, verified bank account | Free — Soundcheck absorbs the fee | 2–3 business days |
| **Card** | Your payout funding method via Stripe | Platform fee shown at confirmation | Per Stripe |

Manual methods — cash or payments made outside Soundcheck — are also available and let you record that a member was paid without moving money through Soundcheck.

## Connecting your bank account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to organization payment settings">
    Open **Settings → Organization → Payments** and find the **Bank Accounts** card. Only an Owner or Admin can add organization source accounts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter account details">
    Provide the account holder name, account type (Checking or Savings), an optional nickname, your 9-digit routing number, and your account number. Soundcheck stores the details encrypted and only ever shows a masked number afterward.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for micro-deposits">
    The account starts as **Pending**. Soundcheck sends two small micro-deposits to your bank account.

    <Warning>
      Micro-deposits typically arrive within **1–2 business days**, though it can take up to 3. Do not attempt to send payouts until the account is verified — Soundcheck will block them and prompt you to complete verification first.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate the amounts">
    When the deposits appear on your bank statement, return to the **Bank Accounts** card and select **Validate Micro Deposit**. Enter the two amounts exactly as they appear, then submit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Account is Verified">
    When the amounts match, the account status flips to **Verified**. Your first verified account is automatically set as the default funding source for direct-deposit payouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Onboarding crew for payouts

Before you can pay a crew member, they need to set up where the money goes. This is self-serve from each member's own profile — you don't manage it on their behalf.

- **Bank account (direct deposit):** The member adds and verifies their own bank account from **Profile → Payments** using the same micro-deposit flow. Once verified, they can receive ACH payouts.
- **Stripe Connect:** The member opens the Stripe Connect card on their profile, clicks **Connect**, and completes Stripe's hosted onboarding (identity, tax details, bank). Their status shows as Setup Incomplete until Stripe activates the account.

Until a member has a verified payout destination, their row in the gig's Finances tab shows a note like *"Member must add a bank account"* and they can't receive a direct-deposit payout.

## Sending your first payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the gig's Finances tab">
    Navigate to the gig and select the **Finances** tab. Each crew member who has accepted with a performance fee appears as a payable row showing their name, position, outstanding amount, and current payout status.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review who's ready">
    Check each row. Members who aren't yet eligible — no bank account added, not yet accepted — show a note explaining why. Resolve any blockers before proceeding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose who to pay">
    Pay a single row, or select **Mark all paid** to run everyone eligible at once. Ineligible rows stay visible so you can fix them without losing track.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the method and confirm">
    Choose **Direct Deposit** or a card funding method. The confirmation summary shows the method, amounts, and any platform fee (card only — ACH is free). Confirm to submit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track to settled">
    Soundcheck updates each payout's status as it moves: **Processing** while ACH settles (2–3 business days), then **Paid** once confirmed. Once every payout on the gig clears, the gig can be closed out and settled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payout statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Unpaid** | No payment submitted yet. |
| **Processing** | Submitted; ACH settlement pending (2–3 business days). |
| **Paid** | Settlement confirmed and recorded. |
| **Failed** | The payout failed. Review the reason and retry. |
| **Voided** | The payout record was cancelled and should not be paid. |

## The org-level Payments page

For a cross-gig view of every payout your organization has sent, open the **Payments** page from your main navigation. It aggregates every payment request and payout with date, recipient, amount, funding method, and the originating gig — with each row linking back to that gig for context.

<Tip>
  Connect [QuickBooks](/integrations/quickbooks) to sync payouts and gig income into your accounting automatically, so reconciliation doesn't require manual exports.
</Tip>
