Updates to Soundcheck, newest first. Ask about any of them in the [support server](https://discord.gg/Yqyh7pVzne).

<Update label="November 24, 2026">

### New features

- `/tracker` song cards: ExoShare links get the green download button (arrows pick the file when a share holds several) and the Play button for voice channels.
- `/download` takes ExoShare links, as does `@Soundcheck <link>` and the "Download media" menu.
- `/play` plays ExoShare links (the share's first audio file).

### Changed

- `/tracker` song cards: Discord, LEAKED.CX, Genius, TIDAL, Amazon Music, Deezer, Filen, Imgur, Internet Archive, MEGA, Bandcamp and TikTok links show their own icon.
- `/download` TikTok posts: the Audio line has the TikTok icon.

### Fixes

- `/tracker` song cards: links to another tab of the tracker (like Ken Carson's "View" links to Social Media) now show as links, named after the tab and row ("Social Media · Row 83"), instead of plain bold text.
- `/tracker` Released cards: trackers with a separate "Streaming Link(s)" column (Ken Carson) now show those links too, so a song with only a Spotify link no longer says "Link Needed".
- `/tracker` song cards: Pillowcase links on its old domains (pillowcase.su, pillowcase.zip, plwcse.top…) now get the download and Play buttons, their videos, and the file's name, like pillows.su links.

</Update>

<Update label="November 22, 2026">

### Changed

- `/play` now-playing card: "Now playing from …" sits on its own line above the song, the picture lines up with the title, and the artist and the era (or the SoundCloud album, or an upload's album tag) each get their own line with an icon, like the Last.fm card.

</Update>

<Update label="November 21, 2026" description="Music in voice channels">

### New features

- `/play` (premium): plays a song in your voice channel. Type a song name to search SoundCloud, or paste a SoundCloud link or a direct link to an audio file. A now-playing card shows the song and a progress bar, with player buttons for everyone in the channel: previous, play/pause, next, stop and Queue.
- `/music nowplaying`, `/music queue`, `/music previous` (back to the start of the song, or to the song before in its first 5 seconds), `/music skip`, `/music pause`, `/music resume` and `/music stop` (premium) control the music. The bot leaves after 3 minutes with nothing playing, or 30 seconds after everyone leaves the channel.
- `/play` has a `file` option: upload an audio or video file (up to 100 MB) to play it. Attaching a file to `@Soundcheck play` works too.
- `/play` has a `tracker` option: pick a tracker and its songs are suggested as you type, then the song plays from its link in the sheet (when several songs or versions can play, pick the one from a menu that lists only those) (Pillowcase, imgur.gg, pixeldrain, Google Drive or SoundCloud). Its card shows the era, the era's picture and colour, and links back to the song in the tracker. Pasting one of those links into `/play` works too.
- `/tracker search` and the other tracker song cards have a Play button that queues the song in your voice channel (premium), when one of its links can be played.
- The invite link now asks for the Connect and Speak permissions that music needs. Servers that added Soundcheck before can give its role those two permissions instead of re-inviting.

### Changed

- `/trackerdle`: Surfaced shows 🟨 when the guess is within 3 months of the answer (Dec 2021 and Jan 2022 are now close), instead of anywhere in the same year, and the board says how close 🟨 means for Surfaced and Length.
- Mentioning the bot with a song link no longer gets a song.link reply. Odesli has retired the API it used.

### Fixes

- `/trackerdle guess`: a very long typed song that isn't in the tracker now gets the "isn't a song" note (shortened) instead of an error.
- Pressing a button, picking from a menu or turning a page no longer pings the person named on the card. Any message the bot updates now stays silent, like the first reply.

</Update>

<Update label="November 17, 2026">

### New features

- `/artist era reset` deletes every saved era of the server's artist in one go, after an "are you sure" step that says how many eras will go. Tracker cards then use the sheet's own era names and colours. Needs Manage Server in the artist's own server, like the other era commands.
- `/trackerdle` shows how long a finished board took ("Time · 2m 14s"), adds it to the Share grid, and `/trackerdle stats` shows your fastest win and average win time for daily and Unlimited. Games played before this have no time.

### Changed

- `/tracker` song cards and Song of the Day show the era in bold with its years when the tracker has no saved era list, for example "**2 Alivë** (2021 - 2022)", read from the era's timeline in the sheet ("(2020)" for a single year, just the name when the sheet gives no years). Eras saved with `/artist era` still come first.
- `/tracker`, `/lastfm`, `/genius`, `/download`, `/media`, `/spotify`, `/soundcloud`, `/youtube`, `/x`, `/tiktok`, `/snippets` and other slow commands show a note saying what they're doing ("Asking Last.fm…", "Downloading…", "Loading the Yeat Tracker…") when the answer takes more than 4 seconds, and the answer replaces it. A command that takes far too long now says "taking too long, try again in a minute" instead of never answering (1 minute for Last.fm, Genius and other outside services, 3 minutes for trackers, 5 minutes for downloads and audio tools, 2 minutes otherwise). A tracker that was still loading keeps loading, so trying again is quick.
- `/download` of a SoundCloud link, `/soundcloud`, `/spotify`, `/lastfm` info cards and Songlink replies answer faster when the same link or lookup was asked for recently (SoundCloud 10 minutes, Spotify and Songlink 1 hour, Last.fm 1 to 10 minutes). Now playing and recent tracks are always live.
- `/help`, `/tracker`, `/trackerdle`, `/snippets`, `/download`, `/youtube`, `/spotify`, `/soundcloud`, `/genius`, `/grail`, games and every other command with buttons or menus answer someone who presses another person's buttons with the same private card: "Only @name can use this. Run the command yourself to get your own." Other private notes (a disabled command, a full Connect Four column, a file that couldn't be sent) are cards now too instead of plain text.
- `/tracker` song cards show pictures from the links column in the card's gallery too, next to videos and in the same order: direct picture links (.jpg, .png, .gif, .webp), imgur.gg picture pages and Pillowcase picture files.

### Fixes

- `/compilation` now says when an artist's Filen folder can't be reached (for example after its password changed) instead of saying the index is still loading. The link card notes that the link or password may be out of date, and the bot tries the folder again every 30 minutes until it works.
- `/trackerdle` daily songs no longer repeat a recent answer: a tracker's song is never one of its answers from the last 3/4 of its pool in days, up to a year (a 20-song pool goes 15 days).
- `/tracker`, `/trackerdle`, `/tracker stats`, `/tracker onthisday` and Song of the Day skip tracker rows with an empty song name, and rows with an empty era when the sheet has an Era column (blank template rows, notes, banners). They no longer show up as songs or count in totals.
- `/tracker` song cards show every link of a cell that holds several hyperlinked lines ("Snippet", "Snippet 2"), instead of the first link repeated. Text without a link in a links column ("LINK NEEDED", "TBA", "?") is no longer shown as a broken link.
- `/tracker` song cards name a link with no readable page title after its site ("SoundCloud", "Pillowcase") or its address ("i.imgur.gg"), instead of error text like "I (Unavailable)" or "HTTP_403".

</Update>

<Update label="November 16, 2026">

### New features

- `/leakedcx setup` opens one card to pick which LEAKED.CX forums post where. You can choose from 20 forums (Rules, Announcements, Groupbuys, every Leaks, Discussion and Creative forum incl. Edits, Remasters, Remakes, Compilations, Images and their request forums), single or with "All Leaks", "All Creative" and other shortcuts. Send them all to one channel or split them across several. Nothing is saved until **Save & Close**. **Reset all** clears every channel at once. Turn on **Post recent threads** to also post the 10 newest threads of each forum you add (up to 60 posts), skipping any thread the server already got.

### Changed

- `/leakedcx add` and `/leakedcx remove` are now `/leakedcx setup`. Your existing feeds carry over, and `/leakedcx status` lists every channel with its forums.
- LEAKED.CX posts keep bold and other formatted text from the thread, which was left out before.

</Update>

<Update label="November 15, 2026">

### New features

- `/trackerdle play` has an Unlimited mode: once today's board is done, keep playing random songs with **Play another**. Unlimited games have their own section in `/trackerdle stats` and never count toward streaks or the leaderboard.
- `/trackerdle` links to a full How to play guide.

### Changed

- Premium commands now start with ⭐ in their description, and options that need Premium (like leaving `tracker` blank to use your default) are marked ⭐ too.
- `/trackerdle play` Unlimited (and **Play another**) now needs server or user Premium. Boards already in progress can still be finished.
- `/trackerdle` hints for era, surfaced date and length now use the version you guessed ("Song [V2]", or the newest version when no tag is typed). Era shows when other versions are in other eras, and autocomplete offers each version.
- `/trackerdle` length is green only when the length is exactly the same.
- `/info bot` is a new card, and the owner is shown as a profile link.
- Card footers use `·` as the separator, and user names at the top of embeds link to their Discord profile.

### Fixes

- `/trackerdle` opens faster on big trackers.
- `/tracker onthisday` and the daily On This Day post no longer list a song as both Revealed and First previewed on the same day.
- `/onthisday` posts now name songs the same way as `/tracker onthisday` (no leading guest artist like "Autumn! - ").

</Update>

<Update label="November 10, 2026" description="Trackerdle and On This Day update">

### New features

- `/trackerdle history` shows your past boards, or every past answer of one tracker.
- `/trackerdle leaderboard` has pages.

### Changed

- Trackerdle treats every version of a song ("[V1]", "[V2]"…) as one guess.
- A Trackerdle board you started and never finished now counts as a loss.
- `/tracker onthisday` says when a song was only revealed (no audio), or leaked and is now lost.

### Fixes

- Daily posts work for servers whose default tracker comes from TrackerHub.
- TrackerHub trackers stay listed when TrackerHub can't be read for a moment.

<Update label="November 8, 2026" description="Stats update">

### New features

- `/onthisday set` posts the day's On This Day card in a channel every day at 12:00 New York time, with an optional role ping (Premium).
- `/tracker stats` shows a tracker's numbers: songs, availability, songs per year, top eras, producers and features.
- `/tracker producer` shows one producer's songs, eras and collaborators.
- `/tracker wrapped` shows a tracker's year: songs that surfaced, OG files, the busiest months.

### Changed

- Slow trackers show a "Loading…" note instead of leaving you waiting.

</Update>

<Update label="November 7, 2026" description="Trackerdle update">

### New features

- `/trackerdle`: guess the day's mystery song from a tracker in 8 tries, with hints for era, credits, date, type and length. Share your result, check `/trackerdle stats` and the server's `/trackerdle leaderboard`.
- `@Soundcheck <link>` downloads the link, and the **Download media** message menu downloads links in any message.

### Changed

- `/identify` is now `/media identify`.
- Text commands only work by mentioning `@Soundcheck`. `/prefix` and AFK are removed.
- Welcome and goodbye messages, autoroles, `/log`, VoiceMaster, leveling and server `/emoji` are removed, and their saved data is deleted. Other bots do these well.
- Song names starting with any emoji are cleaned up in searches, not just a fixed list of emojis.

### Fixes

- Deleting a channel or role removes it from every Soundcheck setting right away.

</Update>
