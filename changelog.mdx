## v2.8 — September 2025

**Rounds digest, CheckAI file ingestion, and QuickBooks sync**

### Rounds

- Launched **Rounds** — a Home-page digest of every open item across all your organization's gigs, bucketed by urgency. Rounds surfaces what needs action **today**, what's due **this week**, and what's worth watching, so nothing slips before doors. Open items include unanswered invitations, unsigned contracts, unpaid payouts, and overdue tasks.
- Rounds updates in real time as crew respond, contracts get signed, and payouts clear — the bucket counts drop as you work through them.
- Rounds is available to Owners and Admins on all plans.

### CheckAI — AI file ingestion

- Introduced **CheckAI file ingestion**: drop a contract, call sheet, rider, or email onto a gig and CheckAI extracts the schedule, crew, ledger lines, and leads into reviewable proposals you commit. Nothing writes to your gig until you confirm.
- Ingestion uses the same **Universal Event Format (UEF)** as the manual importer. The flow is: analyze → review → optional cross-file merge → commit.
- Supported file types: PDF, DOCX, plain-text email (.eml, .txt), and CSV call sheets.
- AI file ingestion is available on Team and Enterprise plans. Contact [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io) to enable it for your organization.

### QuickBooks sync

- **QuickBooks Online integration** is now generally available. Connect your QuickBooks account from **Settings → Organization → Connections** to automatically sync Soundcheck payouts and gig income for reconciliation.
- Payouts sent through Soundcheck create corresponding expense entries in QuickBooks. Income from gig records syncs as revenue.
- Disconnecting preserves all previously synced records in QuickBooks.

### Fixes and improvements

- Gig templates now carry over run-of-show sections and custom fields when duplicated.
- Availability request expiry now closes the check automatically when the gig date passes.
- Mobile Mailbox badge count now reflects only open (unanswered) items, not historical ones.

## v2.5 — August 2025

**MCP server GA, client portal improvements, and touring lineup management**

### MCP server — generally available

- The **Soundcheck MCP server** is now generally available. Connect Claude, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible AI agent to your Soundcheck data through the Model Context Protocol. Point your client at `mcp.soundchecklive.io` and authenticate with your org credentials.
- Agents can read gigs, crew, finances, setlists, and CRM data; create and update events; staff positions; and work the org Mailbox — all in plain language.
- New tools this release: `export_workspace`, `preview_uef_import`, `import_uef` (confirmation-gated), lead pipeline tools (`claim_lead`, `mark_lead_lost`, `convert_lead`), and inventory assign/return.
- MCP server access is available on Team and Enterprise plans.

### Client portal improvements

- **Client portal** now supports **portal forms**: embed lead intake forms, event questionnaires, and post-show feedback forms. Responses flow directly into the matching customer or event record in your CRM.
- Clients can now see a full event timeline in their portal — not just contracts and invoices, but the schedule and key milestones as they happen.
- Portal invitation emails now include a branded header using your organization logo and color settings.
- **White-label branding** for the portal signing experience is now exclusively available on Enterprise.

### Touring lineup management

- New **Managing a touring lineup** tools make it easier to keep a run of shows staffed when the lineup changes leg to leg. Detect conflicts across dates, rotate subs into specific legs, and use call lists per show.
- Tour financial roll-ups now include **per-leg breakdowns**: see revenue, payouts, and net for each leg of a tour as well as the full run.
- Duplicate a gig within a tour to spin up the next date with the same team structure in seconds.

### Fixes and improvements

- Contracts now show a clear DRAFT → SENT → SIGNED/DECLINED status badge on the gig Contracts tab.
- Fixed an issue where cancelling a multi-day gig left open invitation threads in crew members' Mailboxes.
- Gig export PDFs now render with branded headers when white-label branding is enabled.

## v2.2 — July 2025

**Android offline sync, setlist Spotify import, and gig templates for all**

### Android offline sync improvements

- Overhauled the Android offline sync engine. Gig details, setlists, and recently viewed invitations now cache more reliably on Android, including on older devices running Android 10 and 11.
- Queued actions (invitation accepts, availability responses) retry more aggressively when the connection is restored after extended offline periods.
- Fixed an issue where the Android app would occasionally show stale setlist data after reconnecting to a strong signal.
- The **before-the-show checklist** is now surfaced in the mobile app as a pre-show prompt: tap to cache tonight's gig, setlist, and charts while you still have signal.

### Setlist Spotify import

- **Spotify album import** is now available in the setlist builder. Search for an album by name or Spotify URL, and Soundcheck imports the track list into your song library automatically.
- Imported songs include title, artist, and track order. You can reorder, add notes, and attach charts after importing.
- Existing songs in your library are matched by title to avoid duplicates on re-import.

### Gig templates for all plans

- **Save as Template** and the **Templates** tab on the Gigs board are now available on all plans, including Free.
- Templates preserve gig details, team structure, setlists, run of show, and checklist — everything you'd want to reuse for a recurring show.
- Deleted templates are remembered so they're not recreated on next sign-in.

### Fixes and improvements

- Call-time notifications now show the gig timezone instead of the viewer's local device timezone.
- Fixed a display issue on the crew availability calendar where declined offers appeared as available slots.
- Setlist PDF export now respects custom section ordering.
- Org Mailbox now shows a **Replies** view listing every person who responded, newest first, including answers on filled or expired requests.
