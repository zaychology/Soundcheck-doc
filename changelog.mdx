Updates to Soundcheck, newest first. Ask about any of them in the [support server](https://discord.gg/Yqyh7pVzne).

<Update label="November 15, 2026">

### New features

- `/trackerdle play` has an Unlimited mode: once today's board is done, keep playing random songs with **Play another**. Unlimited games have their own section in `/trackerdle stats` and never count toward streaks or the leaderboard.
- `/trackerdle` links to a full How to play guide.

### Changed

- Premium commands now start with ⭐ in their description, and options that need Premium (like leaving `tracker` blank to use your default) are marked ⭐ too.
- `/trackerdle play` Unlimited (and **Play another**) now needs server or user Premium. Boards already in progress can still be finished.
- `/trackerdle` hints for era, surfaced date and length now use the version you guessed ("Song [V2]", or the newest version when no tag is typed). Era shows when other versions are in other eras, and autocomplete offers each version.
- `/trackerdle` length is green only when the length is exactly the same.
- `/info bot` is a new card, and the owner is shown as a profile link.
- Card footers use `·` as the separator, and user names at the top of embeds link to their Discord profile.

### Fixes

- `/trackerdle` opens faster on big trackers.
- `/tracker onthisday` and the daily On This Day post no longer list a song as both Revealed and First previewed on the same day.
- `/onthisday` posts now name songs the same way as `/tracker onthisday` (no leading guest artist like "Autumn! - ").

</Update>

<Update label="November 14, 2026">

### Fixes

- `/artist era` used in DMs now says it only works in a server.

</Update>

<Update label="November 12, 2026">

### Changed

- `/era` is now `/artist era`. Servers that turned `/era` on or off with `/cmd` keep that setting.

</Update>

<Update label="November 11, 2026">

### Fixes

- `/download` no longer fails on links that take a while to fetch.
- `/games uselessfact` answers right away and gives up after 8 seconds instead of hanging.
- `/leakedcx remove` only removes a feed that posts to the channel you name.
- Trackerdle stats are kept when a tracker moves to its TrackerHub copy.
- A tracker whose sheet went private now leaves the `tracker` list within a day.

</Update>

<Update label="November 10, 2026" description="Trackerdle and On This Day update">

### New features

- `/trackerdle history` shows your past boards, or every past answer of one tracker.
- `/trackerdle leaderboard` has pages.

### Changed

- Trackerdle treats every version of a song ("[V1]", "[V2]"…) as one guess.
- A Trackerdle board you started and never finished now counts as a loss.
- `/tracker onthisday` says when a song was only revealed (no audio), or leaked and is now lost.

### Fixes

- Trackerdle keeps every guess, even ones sent quickly one after another.
- Daily posts work for servers whose default tracker comes from TrackerHub.
- TrackerHub trackers stay listed when TrackerHub can't be read for a moment.
- Big trackers no longer slow the bot down while they load.

</Update>

<Update label="November 8, 2026" description="Stats update">

### New features

- `/onthisday set` posts the day's On This Day card in a channel every day at 12:00 New York time, with an optional role ping (Premium).
- `/tracker stats` shows a tracker's numbers: songs, availability, songs per year, top eras, producers and features.
- `/tracker producer` shows one producer's songs, eras and collaborators.
- `/tracker wrapped` shows a tracker's year: songs that surfaced, OG files, the busiest months.

### Changed

- Slow trackers show a "Loading…" note instead of leaving you waiting.

</Update>

<Update label="November 7, 2026" description="Trackerdle update">

### New features

- `/trackerdle`: guess the day's mystery song from a tracker in 8 tries, with hints for era, credits, date, type and length. Share your result, check `/trackerdle stats` and the server's `/trackerdle leaderboard`.
- `@Soundcheck <link>` downloads the link, and the **Download media** message menu downloads links in any message.

### Changed

- `/identify` is now `/media identify`.
- Text commands only work by mentioning `@Soundcheck`. `/prefix` and AFK are removed.
- Welcome and goodbye messages, autoroles, `/log`, VoiceMaster, leveling and server `/emoji` are removed, and their saved data is deleted. Other bots do these well.
- Song names starting with any emoji are cleaned up in searches, not just a fixed list of emojis.

### Fixes

- Deleting a channel or role removes it from every Soundcheck setting right away.

</Update>

<Update label="November 6, 2026">

### New features

- `/tracker onthisday` lists every song that leaked, surfaced or released on a day in any year.

### Fixes

- Big trackers no longer hang while loading.

</Update>

<Update label="November 5, 2026">

### New features

- `/tracker era` finds a tracker's eras straight from the sheet.

### Changed

- Commands named in replies are clickable.

### Fixes

- Curly apostrophes in @mention commands no longer break them.
- The `/lastfm whoknows` title link shows properly.

</Update>

<Update label="November 4, 2026" description="New look">

### Changed

- Soundcheck no longer asks for Administrator when you invite it.
- Admin commands are hidden from members who can't use them.
- `/information` is now `/info`, and `/games` is back.
- `/help` lists commands as clickable links.
- Audio files play in Discord's player again.

</Update>

<Update label="November 3, 2026">

### New features

- `/premium transfer` moves a lifetime server Premium to another server you manage.

### Changed

- The per-artist commands (`/yeat tracker …` and the others) are replaced by `/tracker`, `/snippets` and `/compilation` with a `tracker` or `artist` option.

### Fixes

- Options that take a server, channel or user ID accept every ID, including new long ones.
- `/media cover` adds the Parental Advisory label again.

</Update>
