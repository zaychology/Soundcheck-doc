Pick a plan based on how many people you staff and which tools your operation needs day to day. Every plan includes unlimited gigs, full setlist management, positions, and core team building. Upgrade when you're ready for call lists, finance visibility, CRM, or unlimited scale.

<Note>
  All plans allow you to invite members and create gigs immediately. You only pay when you upgrade to Pro, Team, or Enterprise. Upgrade at any time from **Settings → Organization → Billing**.
</Note>

## What's included on each plan

### Free — Core gig ops for small teams

For organizations that want to run gigs and manage a small roster without a paid plan.

**Included:**
- Unlimited gigs with full event details, timelines, and attachments
- Full setlist builder and reusable song library
- Positions, member invitations, and gig invitations
- Calendar views (month, week, list)
- Up to **5 seats**

**Not included:** call lists, finance roll-ups, crew payments, CRM and leads, admin seats.

---

### Pro — Call lists and financial visibility

For organizations that gig regularly and want saved rosters plus payout visibility.

**Everything in Free, plus:**
- **Call lists**: save priority-ordered rosters (primary + backups) and apply them to a gig in one click
- **Home finance roll-ups**: expected payouts and per-gig breakdowns on your landing page
- **Payments**: pay crew through Soundcheck by ACH direct deposit or card *(requires a finance connection enabled for your org)*
- **1 admin seat** and up to **~15 member seats**

<Card title="Call lists" icon="list-check" href="/organization/call-lists">

</Card>

### Team — CRM, availability, and client portal

For established bands, agencies, and production companies.

**Everything in Pro, plus:**
- **CRM & leads**: capture inquiries, run a pipeline, convert leads to booked gigs; customer, venue, and sponsor directories; inventory tracking
- **Crew availability & offers**: ask availability across your roster, send crew offers, and coordinate schedules
- **Client portal** *(enabled per org)*: give clients a branded space for their event details, contracts, and forms
- **Contracts & e-signatures** *(enabled per org)*: send event agreements for signature, track who's signed
- **5 admin seats** and up to **~50 member seats**
- **Priority support**

For large operations that need custom branding and no seat cap.

**Everything in Team, plus:**
- **White-label branding**: your logo and colors on member-facing surfaces — invitations, emails, and the contract signing experience
- **Unlimited** member and admin seats
- **Custom onboarding** setup with your dedicated contact
- **Priority support** with a dedicated response path

## Choosing a plan

| Your situation | Recommended plan |
| --- | --- |
| Just starting / testing | Free |
| Steady gig calendar, same core lineup | Pro |
| Multiple people managing, or inbound leads | Team |
| Many acts, custom branding, or unlimited scale | Enterprise |

<Tip>
  Not sure? Start on **Free**, then upgrade the moment you hit a seat limit or need call lists. Upgrades are immediate and your data stays intact. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
</Tip>

## Billing and upgrading

Owners manage the subscription from **Settings → Organization → Billing**: change plans, update payment methods, view history, and download invoices. **Manage Subscription** opens the Stripe customer portal.

- **Upgrading** takes effect immediately. New features are available right away.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the next billing cycle. Your data is preserved; features above your new tier become read-only.
