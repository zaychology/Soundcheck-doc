Your Soundcheck subscription controls which features are available to your organization and how many members you can have on your roster. Only the **Owner** can access billing and subscription settings — find them at **Settings → Organization → Billing**.

<Warning>
  Billing lives at **Settings → Organization → Billing**, not on the personal Settings page. The personal `/settings` page covers appearance only and has no Billing or Organization section.
</Warning>

---

## Where to manage billing

Open **Settings → Organization → Billing** to:

- View your current plan and billing cycle
- See your seat usage (members + pending invitations)
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Add or remove seats
- Update your payment method
- Download invoices and receipts
- Access the **Manage Subscription** link, which opens the Stripe customer portal

---

## Upgrade your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Organization → Billing**. You'll see your current plan, billing cycle, and next renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new plan">
    Click **Upgrade** and choose the plan you want: Pro, Team, or Enterprise. A summary of what's included appears before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and pay">
    Review the prorated charge for the remainder of your current billing cycle and confirm. For Enterprise, select **Contact us** to get a custom quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using new features">
    Your upgrade takes effect immediately. New features — call lists, CRM, CheckAI, white-label branding — are available as soon as checkout completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Upgrades are immediate and prorated. You pay only for the days remaining in your current billing period at the new plan's rate.
</Tip>

## Downgrade your plan

To move to a lower tier, open **Settings → Organization → Billing** and click **Manage Subscription** to open the Stripe customer portal. Select a lower plan and confirm the downgrade.

**What to know:**
- Downgrades take effect at the **next billing cycle**, not immediately. You retain access to current features until then.
- Your data is preserved. Features from the higher tier become **read-only** after the downgrade (for example, you can view existing call lists but can't create new ones on Free).
- If the downgrade puts you over the lower plan's seat limit, existing members stay, but you won't be able to invite new ones until you're back under the cap.

## Billing cycles: monthly and annual

Soundcheck offers both **monthly** and **annual** billing:

- **Monthly**: billed each month, cancel anytime with no commitment
- **Annual**: billed once per year at a discounted rate — a meaningful saving for teams that know they'll be using Soundcheck year-round

Switch between monthly and annual from **Settings → Organization → Billing → Manage Subscription** in the Stripe portal.

## Adding seats

Seat limits are set by your plan tier (see [Plans](/subscriptions/plans)). If you're on Team or Pro and need more seats than your plan includes, you have two options:

1. **Upgrade to a higher plan** — more seats come included
2. **Custom seat limit** — some organizations have a per-org seat limit configured that overrides the plan default. Contact [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io) to discuss a custom arrangement

Your Members tab always shows the seat limit that actually applies to you, including any custom overrides.

## Payment methods

Soundcheck accepts all major **credit and debit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) for subscription billing. Payments are processed securely through **Stripe**.

To update your payment method:

1. Go to **Settings → Organization → Billing**
2. Click **Manage Subscription** to open the Stripe customer portal
3. Update your card details and save

Receipts are automatically emailed after each payment. You can also download past invoices directly from **Settings → Organization → Billing**.

<Note>
  Subscription billing (your Soundcheck plan) is separate from **gig payout funding** (paying crew). Payout funding methods are configured at **Settings → Organization → Connections**, not in Billing.
</Note>

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

1. Go to **Settings → Organization → Billing → Manage Subscription** in the Stripe portal
2. Select your current plan and choose **Cancel subscription**
3. Confirm the cancellation

**After cancellation:**
- Monthly plans: access continues through the end of the current billing period
- Annual plans: access continues through the end of the paid annual term
- Your organization is downgraded to the **Free plan** — all your data is preserved
- Premium features become read-only; you cannot create new ones but can view existing records

**Refunds:** Monthly plans are non-refundable. For annual plans, contact support within 14 days of purchase for refund eligibility.

## Subscription vs. gig payouts

It's worth understanding the distinction between paying for Soundcheck and paying your crew through Soundcheck:

| | Subscription billing | Gig payouts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is** | Payment for your Soundcheck plan | Payments to crew for performing |
| **Managed at** | Settings → Organization → Billing | Settings → Organization → Connections |
| **Who manages it** | Owner only | Owner (with finance access) |
| **Payment method** | Credit/debit card (via Stripe) | Verified bank account or card |

## Enterprise pricing

Enterprise plans are custom-quoted based on your team size, feature needs, and support requirements. To get a quote:

- Email [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io)
- Include your organization size, the features you need, and any custom requirements (SSO, dedicated support, custom onboarding)

Enterprise quotes typically include unlimited seats, white-label branding, priority support, and a custom onboarding call with the Soundcheck team.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade mid-cycle?">
    Yes. Upgrades are immediate and prorated — you'll be charged only for the days remaining in the current billing period at the new plan's rate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my payment fails?">
    Soundcheck notifies you and retries the payment. If payment continues to fail, your subscription may be downgraded to Free after a grace period. Update your payment method promptly at **Settings → Organization → Billing** to avoid interruption.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do all my members need their own subscription?">
    No. The subscription is per organization. All members get access to whatever features the org's plan includes — they don't pay separately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on the number of gigs?">
    No. Plans limit **seats** and **features**, not the number of gigs you can create. You can have an unlimited number of gigs on any plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I lose my data if I cancel?">
    No. Cancelling downgrades you to the Free plan and preserves all your data. Premium features become read-only rather than deleted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
