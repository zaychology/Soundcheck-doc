## Notes on specific features

**Payments** require a Pro or higher plan *and* a financial connection enabled for your organization (set up under **Settings → Organization → Connections**). Finance roll-ups are only visible to Owners and Admins — not Gig Coordinators or Members.

**Contracts and client portal** are enabled per organization on Team and are available by default on Enterprise. If you're on Team and don't see them, contact [info@soundchecklive.io](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io).

**CheckAI and MCP server** are available on Team and Enterprise. MCP server connect URLs are published once DNS is confirmed for your org.

**CRM features** (customer and venue directories, lead pipeline) require Team or Enterprise. Customer and venue sidebar links may stay hidden until CRM is enabled for your org.
