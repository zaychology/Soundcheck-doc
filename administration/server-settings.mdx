## Choose where commands work

These need Manage Server.

- `/cmd disable` and `/cmd enable` turn one command off or on for the whole server. `/cmd channel` turns it off in one channel, `/cmd member` for one member, and `/cmd list` shows what's off.
- `/module` does the same for a whole category, such as Last.fm or Levels.
- `/bypass addrole` and `/bypass addmember` let a role or member ignore these limits. `/bypass list` shows them.

## Prefix and settings

These work by mentioning Soundcheck, and need Administrator to change.

- `@Soundcheck prefix` shows the prefix. `prefix set` and `prefix remove` change it.
- `@Soundcheck settings` shows every setting for the server.
- `@Soundcheck resetsettings` resets everything. It can't be undone.

## Song of the Day

`/songoftheday set <channel> <role>` posts a song from your artist's tracker every day at 12:00 New York time, with a ping for the role. `/songoftheday config` shows the setup, `/songoftheday run` posts one now, and `/songoftheday remove` turns it off. Needs [Premium](/administration/premium) and Administrator.

## LEAKED.CX feed

`@Soundcheck leakedcx add <channel> <feed>` posts new LEAKED.CX threads to a channel. `leakedcx status` shows your feeds and `leakedcx remove` stops one. Needs Administrator.

## Bot profile

`/customize avatar`, `banner` and `bio` give Soundcheck its own look in your server. `/customize reset` puts it back. Needs Premium and Administrator.

## Welcome, goodbye and autoroles

`/welcome`, `/goodbye` and `/autorole` save messages and roles for members who join or leave.

<Warning>
These don't run right now. They need Soundcheck to see members joining and leaving, and Discord hasn't given Soundcheck that permission yet. Your settings are kept for when it does.
</Warning>
