## Choose where commands work

These need Manage Server.

- `/cmd disable` and `/cmd enable` turn one command off or on for the whole server. `/cmd channel` turns it off in one channel, `/cmd member` for one member, and `/cmd list` shows what's off.
- `/module` does the same for a whole category, such as Last.fm or Fun.
- `/bypass addrole` and `/bypass addmember` let a role or member ignore these limits. `/bypass list` shows them.

## Settings

These need Administrator.

- `/settings view` shows the server's default artist, Song of the Day channel and LEAKED.CX feeds.
- `/settings reset` resets the server's settings (Premium is kept). It can't be undone.

## Song of the Day

`/songoftheday set <channel> [role]` posts a song from your artist's tracker every day at 12:00 New York time, with an optional ping for the role. `/songoftheday config` shows the setup, `/songoftheday run` posts one now, and `/songoftheday remove` turns it off. Needs [Premium](/administration/premium) and Administrator.

## On This Day

`/onthisday set <channel> [role]` posts the day's `/tracker onthisday` card for your server's artist every day at 12:00 New York time. Days with nothing on them are skipped. `/onthisday config` shows the setup, `/onthisday run` posts one now, and `/onthisday remove` turns it off. Needs [Premium](/administration/premium) and Administrator.

## LEAKED.CX feed

`/leakedcx setup` opens a card where you pick which LEAKED.CX forums post new threads to which channels. Pick a channel, then tick its forums in the two menus. Shortcuts like "All Leaks" or "All Creative" tick a whole group. A channel can get any number of forums, and a forum can post in several channels (a thread is posted once per channel). Changes are only saved with **Save & Close**; **Cancel**, or 10 minutes without a click, keeps everything as it was. To turn a channel off, pick it and press **Remove this channel**, then save. **Reset all** removes every channel at once (also only after **Save & Close**).

Forums you can follow: Rules, Announcements & Site Updates, Groupbuys, Hip-Hop / Other Genre / Other Media Leaks, Hip-Hop / Other Genre Discussion, Edits, Remasters, Remaster Requests, Remakes, Remake Requests, Edit Requests, AI Edits, Compilations, Compilation Requests, Beats and Music, Images and Image Requests. Only new threads are posted, never replies. Turn on **Post recent threads** before saving to also post the 10 newest threads of each forum you just added (up to 60 posts per save). Threads your server already got, in any channel, are skipped.

`/leakedcx status` lists every channel with its forums. Needs Administrator.

## Bot profile

`/customize avatar`, `banner` and `bio` give Soundcheck its own look in your server. `/customize reset` puts it back. Needs Premium and Administrator.
