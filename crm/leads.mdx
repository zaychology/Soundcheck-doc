The leads pipeline is where new business starts. Every inbound inquiry — whether it came through your public booking form, a phone call, or an email — lives here as a lead. You move each one through stages as you qualify and follow up, then convert the confirmed bookings into gigs and customer records in a single action. Nothing falls through the cracks between "they reached out" and "it's on the calendar."

<Info>
  Leads live under **Business → Leads** in the sidebar and are available on **Team** and **Enterprise** plans. On **Free**, the Leads section may appear with a lock icon. Pro does not include Leads. If you don't see Leads, [contact support](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io).
</Info>

## Open the pipeline

Navigate to **Business → Leads** in the sidebar. The **Pipeline** page opens on the **Leads** tab. A **Sponsors** tab sits alongside it for sponsor relationships — there is no separate Sponsors sidebar item.

The top of the page shows four at-a-glance metrics:

| Metric | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Total** | All inbound requests ever received |
| **Open** | Requests still in progress (New, Qualified, or Quoted) |
| **Won** | Requests converted to gigs |
| **Lost** | Requests marked as not converting |

Filter the list by stage and search by name, contact, location, or event type.

## Lead stages

Leads move through a pipeline from first contact to a closed outcome:

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | A fresh inquiry awaiting first follow-up. |
| **Qualified** | You've confirmed the opportunity is viable. |
| **Quoted** | You've shared pricing or a proposal. |
| **Won** | The lead was converted to a gig (closed). |
| **Lost** | The opportunity didn't convert (closed). |

Move open leads between **New**, **Qualified**, and **Quoted** using the stage dropdown on any lead record. Use **Convert to Gig** to close a lead as **Won**, or **Mark lost** when it won't convert.

<Note>
  Once a lead is converted, its card shows a **Gig Created** badge linking to the new gig and a **Customer** badge linking to the customer record it created or reused. Won and Lost leads are closed and can no longer be edited or converted.
</Note>

## What a lead tracks

Each lead record holds the details your team needs to qualify and follow up:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Name | Who made the inquiry |
| Email and phone | How to follow up |
| Event type | Wedding, corporate, private event, and more |
| Event date | When the event might happen |
| Location | Where the event may take place |
| Budget | Expected spend or quoted amount |
| Message | The inquiry's own note or details |

Open any lead to see its full details, submission timestamp, current stage, and a space to add follow-up notes.

## Capture a new lead

### Add a lead manually

Use **Create Lead** when an inquiry arrives by phone, email, referral, or social media. Fill in what you know — even a name and phone number is enough to start — and update the record as you learn more.

### Collect leads from your public intake form

On Team and Enterprise, your organization has a public booking request form prospects can fill out without a Soundcheck login. Submissions create leads automatically and appear in your pipeline immediately.

From the Leads pipeline, use these two actions to share the form:

- **Open Intake Form** — opens your public form in a new tab so you can copy the link or test it yourself.
- **Copy Embed** — copies an `<iframe>` snippet you can paste directly into your website.

The public form collects contact name, email, phone, event type, potential date, location, budget, and an inquiry message.

<Tip>
  Add your intake form link to your website's booking page, your email signature, and your social media profiles so inquiries flow in automatically rather than landing in scattered inboxes.
</Tip>

<Note>
  You can also collect leads through branded portal forms built with JotForm or Form.io. Any form set to the **lead intake** purpose routes responses into this same pipeline. See [portal forms](/crm/leads).
</Note>

## Qualify and convert a lead into a gig

When a lead is ready to become a real booking, convert it. Soundcheck handles the CRM-to-gig handoff automatically — creating the gig, the customer, and the contact link in one action.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the lead">
    In **Business → Leads**, open a lead at any open stage (New, Qualified, or Quoted), or click **Convert** directly on the lead's card in the pipeline list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pre-filled gig">
    The gig creation form opens pre-populated with details from the lead. The title defaults to `{event type} - {name}` (for example, *Wedding - Jane Smith*). The date, location, and client message carry over into the gig notes. Adjust any field before confirming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm to create">
    Click to confirm. Soundcheck creates a new gig, creates or reuses a matching customer record, adds a primary contact from the lead's contact information, and links the customer to the gig. The lead stage moves to **Won**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue setup from the gig">
    Soundcheck takes you straight to the new gig's detail page. From here, add crew, setlists, tasks, a venue, contracts, and payment details to finish preparing the event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Conversion does **not** automatically send crew invitations, collect payments, or finalize setlists. Use the normal gig workflow after conversion to complete those steps.
</Info>

### Customer matching during conversion

Before creating a new customer, Soundcheck checks your directory for an existing record that matches the lead's contact information. If one exists, it reuses that record and adds the new gig to the existing history. If nothing matches, it creates a new customer and primary contact. This keeps your directory clean without requiring you to manually de-duplicate new bookings.

## HubSpot and Flex pipeline integration

Leads created in Soundcheck — via **Create Lead** or your public intake form — are managed manually through the stages above. When you're ready to book, you convert them by hand.

For teams whose sales process already lives in **HubSpot** or **Flex**, there's a separate path. Through the [HubSpot ↔ Flex pipeline integration](/integrations/overview), deals and quotes from those systems sync into Soundcheck via an n8n webhook and can open a **draft gig** directly — with deal stages kept in lockstep as the opportunity advances. These synced opportunities don't wait in the Leads pipeline for a manual conversion.

Use Leads for inquiries you manage inside Soundcheck. Use the HubSpot ↔ Flex integration when your primary sales workflow runs in those platforms.

## Best practices

- Review open leads regularly and follow up while the inquiry is still warm.
- Add notes after each conversation so your team has context when they pick up the thread.
- Update the stage as you go — a glance at the pipeline should show exactly where each opportunity stands.
- Convert only once the event details are firm enough to create a real gig.
- After converting, use [customers](/crm/customers) and [venues](/crm/venues) to keep repeat client work organized.
