Soundcheck CRM is the business layer behind your gig operation. It keeps your clients, venues, sponsors, and gear organized in one place — and connects every record to the gigs they're tied to. Rather than juggling contacts across spreadsheets and email threads, your whole team works from the same shared directory. CRM features are available on **Team** and **Enterprise** plans.

## Who the CRM is for

CRM is designed for the person running your booking operation — typically an **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Gig Coordinator**. If you manage repeat clients, work across multiple venues, or handle inbound booking inquiries in volume, the CRM gives you a structured place to keep all of that instead of relying on memory or a separate tool.

<Info>
  CRM features — Customers, Venues, Sponsors, Inventory, and Leads — are available on **Team** and **Enterprise** plans. On **Free** and **Pro**, these sections may appear with a lock icon or be hidden. If you don't see CRM in your sidebar, check your plan or [contact support](mailto:info@soundchecklive.io).
</Info>

## How CRM records connect to gigs

Every CRM record is designed to feed your gig workflow, not sit separately from it:

- **Customers** link to gigs via the gig's **Clients** tab, giving you a running history of every event you've done for each client.
- **Venues** link to gigs via the gig's **Venue** tab, automatically flowing load-in details, contacts, and access info onto each show.
- **Leads** convert directly into gigs — Soundcheck creates the gig, a customer record, and a primary contact in a single action.
- **Inventory** items can be assigned to specific gigs so you always know what gear is out and what's available.

This means the work you do in your CRM directly populates your gigs. You enter a venue once and every crew member on every future show at that location sees the right load-in details without anyone retyping them.

## The lead-to-gig workflow

The CRM's leads pipeline is where new business starts. An inquiry comes in — through your public intake form, a phone call, email, or referral — and becomes a lead. You qualify it, follow up, and when it's ready to book, convert it. Soundcheck handles the handoff automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the inquiry">
    Inbound requests land in **Business → Leads** when submitted through your public intake form. Add inquiries that arrive by phone or email using **Create Lead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it through the pipeline">
    Update the lead's stage — **New**, **Qualified**, or **Quoted** — as you follow up and gather details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert to a gig">
    When the booking is confirmed, use **Convert to Gig**. Soundcheck creates a new gig pre-filled from the lead, creates or reuses a customer record, and links everything together. The lead moves to **Won**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the gig">
    Continue setup from the new gig — add crew, setlists, contracts, and payment details — with the client already connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>
